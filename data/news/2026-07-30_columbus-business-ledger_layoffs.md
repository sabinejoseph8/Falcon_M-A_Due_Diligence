# Talonfield to cut 4% of staff in latest restructuring

> FICTIONAL ARTICLE created for a portfolio demonstration. The outlet and all people quoted are invented.

- Outlet: Columbus Business Ledger (fictional)
- Date: July 30, 2026
- Topic: Restructuring

Talonfield Systems will cut about 60 jobs, or 4% of its workforce, mostly in hardware assembly and field services, according to a notice filed with the state.

It is the company's fourth restructuring program since 2022. Each year the company has excluded restructuring costs from its Adjusted EBITDA as "not part of ongoing operations."

A company spokesperson said the cuts would "align our cost base with near-term demand."
