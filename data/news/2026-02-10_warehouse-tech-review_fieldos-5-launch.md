# Talonfield launches FieldOS 5 with AI route planning, adds three enterprise customers

- Outlet: Warehouse Tech Review (fictional)

Talonfield Systems released version 5 of FieldOS, its fleet management software, adding AI-based route planning that the company says can raise picking productivity by up to 18%.

The company also announced three new enterprise agreements, including an expansion with Harbor & Vine Grocers to 12 additional distribution centers. The other two customers were not named.

"FieldOS is becoming the operating system of the warehouse," Chief Executive Marcus Hale said at the company's customer conference in Las Vegas. Hale said software would grow to "more than a third of revenue" within three years.

Analysts at the event said customer feedback on FieldOS 5 was positive, although several noted that the new features require Talon Gen-3 robots or newer.
