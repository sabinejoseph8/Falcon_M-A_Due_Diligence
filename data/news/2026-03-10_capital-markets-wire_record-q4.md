# Talonfield posts record fourth quarter, guides 2026 revenue to about $560 million

- Outlet: Capital Markets Wire (fictional)
- Date: March 10, 2026
- Topic: Earnings

Talonfield Systems reported fourth-quarter revenue of $154.4 million, up 32% from a year earlier and well above analyst expectations of about $128 million. Full-year revenue rose 19% to $502.3 million.

The company guided 2026 revenue to $555 million to $565 million and Adjusted EBITDA to $114 million to $122 million, and repeated the targets at its investor day.

Shares rose 9% to $31.20, near their 52-week high.

Some analysts questioned the quality of the quarter. "Receivables jumped and operating cash flow fell to under $10 million for the year," wrote one analyst in a note to clients. "We want to see the fourth-quarter shipments turn into cash before we get more constructive."

The company also disclosed that its new auditor had issued an adverse opinion on internal controls because of the material weakness first reported in November.
