# Talonfield cuts 2026 outlook as revenue falls; interim CFO calls it a "reset year"

> FICTIONAL ARTICLE created for a portfolio demonstration. The outlet and all people quoted are invented.

- Outlet: Capital Markets Wire (fictional)
- Date: August 7, 2026
- Topic: Earnings

Talonfield Systems cut its 2026 revenue outlook to $470 million to $490 million, from $555 million to $565 million, and its Adjusted EBITDA outlook to $70 million to $78 million, from $114 million to $122 million.

Second-quarter revenue fell 4% to $112.9 million. The company said the quarter included a $9.4 million credit after a customer returned robots that had been sold under a bill-and-hold arrangement in late 2025, and $2.3 million of credits to customers affected by the April FieldOS outage.

"2026 is a reset year," interim Chief Financial Officer Rachel Simmons said on the earnings call.

Two brokers downgraded the stock. Shares fell 14% to $16.40.
