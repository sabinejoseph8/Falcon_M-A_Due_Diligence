## The deal

- **Buyer:** Arbelion Industries, Inc. ("Arbelion"), a diversified industrial group with FY2025 revenue of $6.2 billion. Strategic goal: enter the warehouse automation market.
- **Target:** Talonfield Systems, Inc. ("Talonfield", NASDAQ: TLFS), headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. Talonfield makes autonomous mobile robots (Talon AMR), sells the FieldOS fleet software subscription, and provides installation and maintenance services.
- **Proposed price:** $24.00 per share in cash, a 34% premium to the October 16, 2026 closing price of $17.85.
- **Diluted shares:** 48.3 million. Implied equity value: about $1,159 million.
- **Structure:** one-step merger, financed with Arbelion cash on hand and a new Arbelion term loan.
- **Exclusivity:** expires November 20, 2026.
- **Investment Committee (IC) meeting:** November 6, 2026.

## Valuation basis used in the current deal model

The deal model (v3, September 2026) uses the management case presented at Talonfield's March 2026 investor day:

- FY2026E revenue: $560 million
- FY2026E Adjusted EBITDA: $118 million
- Implied EV / FY2026E Adjusted EBITDA: about 11.5x (using Talonfield net debt at June 30, 2026)
- Run-rate cost synergies: $35 million by year 3

## Questions from the Investment Committee

1. Is Talonfield's revenue growth real and sustainable?
2. Are there legal exposures that could reduce value or block closing?
3. How dependent is Talonfield on key customers, and are those relationships at risk?
4. Are there balance sheet, debt or liquidity issues we would inherit at closing?
5. Are there management, governance or internal control concerns?
6. What price adjustments or deal protections should we ask for?
