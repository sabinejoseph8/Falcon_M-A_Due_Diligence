# Financial data supplement (fictional)

> FICTIONAL DATA created for a portfolio demonstration. Talonfield Systems is not a real company.

US$ millions unless stated. Fiscal year = calendar year. H1 = six months ended June 30. LTM = last twelve months.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `income_statement.csv` | Annual income statements FY2022 to FY2025, plus H1 2025 and H1 2026 |
| `revenue_quarterly.csv` | Quarterly revenue, Q1 2024 to Q2 2026 |
| `balance_sheet.csv` | Balance sheets at Dec 31, 2023, 2024, 2025 and June 30, 2026 |
| `cash_flow.csv` | Cash flow statements FY2023 to FY2025, plus H1 2025 and H1 2026 |
| `adjusted_ebitda_reconciliation.csv` | Company-defined Adjusted EBITDA and the add-backs behind it |
| `revenue_by_customer.csv` | Top 10 customers, FY2024 and FY2025 |
| `kpis.csv` | Operating metrics (software ARR, retention, backlog, headcount) |

Source: company financial data supplement and SEC filings (simulated). The deal team has not yet received a quality of earnings report.
