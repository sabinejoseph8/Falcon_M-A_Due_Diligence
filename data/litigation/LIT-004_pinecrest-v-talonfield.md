# LIT-004: Pinecrest Distribution LLC v. Talonfield Systems, Inc.

> FICTIONAL COURT RECORD created for a portfolio demonstration. Parties and filings are invented.

- Court: Franklin County (Ohio) Court of Common Pleas
- Case number: 26CV004417 (fictional)
- Filed: June 17, 2026
- Nature of suit: Breach of contract (FieldOS subscription service level agreement) and negligence
- Amount claimed: $12.0 million (lost profits, overtime labor and expedited shipping costs from the April 2026 outage)
- Status: Active. Pleadings stage.

## Key facts from the complaint and answer

- Pinecrest's FieldOS subscription guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime. The April 11 to 14, 2026 outage stopped robots at four Pinecrest distribution centers for about three days.
- Pinecrest says the outage was caused by Talonfield's failure to patch a known vulnerability, and argues this was gross negligence, which would remove the contract's limitation of liability.
- Talonfield's answer (filed August 1, 2026) says its liability is capped by contract at 12 months of subscription fees, which it estimates at about $1.4 million.
