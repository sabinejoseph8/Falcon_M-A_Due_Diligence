# LIT-001: Vantor Robotics, Inc. v. Talonfield Systems, Inc.

> FICTIONAL COURT RECORD created for a portfolio demonstration. Parties, patents and filings are invented.

- Court: U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware
- Case number: 1:24-cv-00318 (fictional)
- Filed: March 11, 2024
- Nature of suit: Patent infringement
- Patents asserted: three Vantor patents (the '557, '812 and '094 patents) covering LiDAR-based robot localization and multi-robot path planning
- Accused products: Talon Gen-3 robots; Talon Gen-3 Plus added August 2026
- Relief sought: damages (complaint: "not less than $140 million"), enhanced damages for willful infringement, and a permanent injunction
- Status: Active. Post claim construction. Trial date set.

## Docket summary

| Date | Entry |
|---|---|
| 2024-03-11 | Complaint filed. Vantor alleges willful infringement of the '557, '812 and '094 patents by Talon Gen-3 robots. |
| 2024-05-20 | Talonfield answer and counterclaims, asserting non-infringement and invalidity. |
| 2024-11-04 | Talonfield files petitions for inter partes review (IPR) at the Patent Trial and Appeal Board (PTAB) challenging the '557 and '812 patents. |
| 2025-05-15 | PTAB denies institution of IPR on the '557 patent. PTAB institutes IPR on the '812 patent. |
| 2025-09-30 | Scheduling order. Jury trial set for March 8, 2027. |
| 2026-07-22 | **Claim construction (Markman) order.** The court adopts Vantor's proposed construction for three of the four disputed terms, including "localization map" in the '557 patent. Commentators note that "localization map" is central to how the Talon Gen-3 navigation system works. |
| 2026-08-14 | Vantor serves amended infringement contentions adding the Talon Gen-3 Plus variant. |
| 2026-09-04 | Vantor serves its damages expert report (filed under seal). A public letter to the court states that Vantor's expert calculates reasonable-royalty damages of $96.4 million through the trial date, and that Vantor will seek a permanent injunction against sales of Talon Gen-3 robots. |
| 2026-09-25 | Court denies Talonfield's motion to stay the case pending the PTAB decision on the '812 patent. A PTAB final written decision on the '812 patent is expected in November 2026. The '557 and '094 patents are not affected by the IPR. |
| 2026-10-05 | Court orders the parties to mediation. Mediation scheduled for December 2026. |
