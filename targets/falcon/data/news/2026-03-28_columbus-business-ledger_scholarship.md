Talonfield Systems will fund 30 scholarships a year for students studying robotics and mechatronics at three Ohio community colleges, the company said on Friday. The five-year program is worth about $1.5 million.

"Our growth depends on skilled technicians," said Priya Raman, Talonfield's chief operating officer. The company employs about 1,450 people, most of them in Ohio.
