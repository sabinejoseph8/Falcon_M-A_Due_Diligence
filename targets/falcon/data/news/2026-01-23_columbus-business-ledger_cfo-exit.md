# Talonfield CFO's exit followed boardroom clash over year-end deals, people familiar say

The abrupt resignation of Talonfield Systems' chief financial officer, Daniel Okafor, followed weeks of tension with Chief Executive Marcus Hale over how to book several large fourth-quarter hardware orders, according to two people familiar with the matter who asked not to be named because the discussions were private.

One of the people said Okafor had argued that revenue on some orders should not be recognized until the robots were delivered to customer sites in 2026. The other said the disagreement was "about timing, not about whether the orders were real."

Talonfield's regulatory filing on Wednesday said Okafor's departure "was not the result of any disagreement with the Company." A company spokesperson repeated that statement on Thursday and declined to comment further. Okafor could not be reached for comment.

The filing also showed that Okafor will receive a $900,000 separation payment. Compensation consultants said separation payments are uncommon when an executive resigns voluntarily.

Rachel Simmons, the company's corporate controller, will serve as interim CFO.
