# Warehouse robotics market to grow 15% a year through 2030, report says

> FICTIONAL ARTICLE created for a portfolio demonstration. The outlet, the research firm and all figures are invented.

- Outlet: Warehouse Tech Review (fictional)
- Date: August 20, 2026
- Topic: Industry

Spending on autonomous mobile robots for warehouses in North America will grow about 15% a year through 2030, according to a new report from industry research firm Calder Insights.

The report ranks Talonfield Systems third in North America with about 11% market share, behind two larger rivals, including Vantor Robotics. Calder said Talonfield's FieldOS software "remains a differentiator," but warned that large retailers are increasingly developing robots in-house.
