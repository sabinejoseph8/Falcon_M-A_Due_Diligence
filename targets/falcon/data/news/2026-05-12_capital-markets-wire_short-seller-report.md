# Short seller Greyfin targets Talonfield over "bill-and-hold" sales; shares sink 18%

> FICTIONAL ARTICLE created for a portfolio demonstration. The outlet, the research firm and all people quoted are invented.

- Outlet: Capital Markets Wire (fictional)
- Date: May 12, 2026
- Topic: Short seller allegations

Shares of Talonfield Systems fell 18% on Tuesday after short seller Greyfin Research published a report titled "Holding the Bag," alleging that the company used bill-and-hold arrangements to meet its 2025 revenue targets.

Greyfin, which disclosed that it is betting against Talonfield's stock, alleged that:

- "Hundreds" of robots booked as fourth-quarter sales were still sitting in Talonfield's Columbus warehouse in February 2026, based on photographs and interviews with two former warehouse employees.
- Talonfield's largest customer did not ask for the bill-and-hold arrangement. Greyfin cited one former Talonfield sales employee.
- Without the bill-and-hold sales, Talonfield would have missed the revenue target in its executive bonus plan.

Talonfield called the report "misleading and inaccurate" and said its revenue recognition "complies with GAAP and was reviewed by our independent auditor." The company said it would not comment on its customers.

The stock closed at $19.85, down from a 52-week high of $31.75 in March.
