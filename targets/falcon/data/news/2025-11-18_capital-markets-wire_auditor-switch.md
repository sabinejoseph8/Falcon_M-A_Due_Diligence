# Talonfield swaps auditor weeks before year-end

> FICTIONAL ARTICLE created for a portfolio demonstration. The outlet and all people quoted are invented.

- Outlet: Capital Markets Wire (fictional)
- Date: November 18, 2025
- Topic: Accounting / governance

Talonfield Systems (NASDAQ: TLFS) has replaced Hartwell & Pryce, its auditor since 2016, with Linden Barr LLP, a Cleveland-based regional firm that audits fewer than ten publicly listed companies.

In a regulatory filing on Friday, Talonfield said it had no disagreements with Hartwell & Pryce. The filing also disclosed that the outgoing auditor had flagged a "material weakness" in controls over certain hardware contracts in the third quarter and had warned that it would need to expand its audit work on fourth-quarter deals with unusual delivery terms.

"Changing auditors in mid-November, with the year-end audit about to start, is unusual," said a professor of accounting who reviewed the filing. "Investors will want to know why the company did not want the expanded procedures."

A Talonfield spokesperson said the change followed "a routine review to find the best fit for a growing company" and that Linden Barr "brings deep experience with industrial technology clients."

Talonfield shares closed down 2.1% on Monday.
