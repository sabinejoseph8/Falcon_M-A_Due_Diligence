## Item 4.01 Changes in Registrant's Certifying Accountant

**(a) Dismissal of previous independent registered public accounting firm.** On November 10, 2025, the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors dismissed Hartwell & Pryce LLP as the Company's independent registered public accounting firm. Hartwell & Pryce had audited the Company's financial statements since 2016.

Hartwell & Pryce's reports on the Company's financial statements for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023 did not contain an adverse opinion or a disclaimer of opinion, and were not qualified or modified as to uncertainty, audit scope or accounting principles.

During the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023, and the subsequent interim period through November 10, 2025, there were no disagreements with Hartwell & Pryce on any matter of accounting principles or practices, financial statement disclosure, or auditing scope or procedure.

**Reportable event.** During the third quarter of 2025, Hartwell & Pryce advised the Audit Committee of a material weakness in the Company's internal control over financial reporting related to the review of hardware contracts with non-standard delivery and acceptance terms. Hartwell & Pryce also advised the Audit Committee that, in its view, additional audit procedures would be required for revenue arrangements with these terms expected in the fourth quarter of 2025, which would expand the scope of its audit.

**(b) Engagement of new independent registered public accounting firm.** On November 12, 2025, the Audit Committee engaged Linden Barr LLP as the Company's independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2025.

The Company provided Hartwell & Pryce with a copy of this disclosure. Hartwell & Pryce furnished a letter stating that it agrees with the statements in this Item 4.01 (Exhibit 16.1).
