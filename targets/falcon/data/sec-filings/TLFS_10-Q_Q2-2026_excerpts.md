**Revenue.** Second-quarter revenue decreased 4.0%. Q2 2026 revenue includes a $9.4 million reduction related to a contract amendment with our largest customer (see "Bill-and-hold amendment" below) and $2.3 million of service level credits issued to FieldOS customers following the April 2026 service disruption. Hardware revenue was also affected by the timing of customer deployments and by orders that customers pulled forward into the fourth quarter of 2025.

**Bill-and-hold amendment.** In May 2026 we amended a purchase order with our largest customer. Under the amendment, products with a sales value of $9.4 million that we had recognized as revenue under a bill-and-hold arrangement in the fourth quarter of 2025 were returned to our inventory, and we issued a credit for the same amount. We recorded the credit as a reduction of revenue in the second quarter of 2026. The remaining $22.4 million of products recognized under bill-and-hold arrangements in the fourth quarter of 2025 had been delivered to customers as of June 30, 2026.

**April 2026 service disruption.** On April 11, 2026, we detected unauthorized activity in part of the FieldOS cloud environment. As a precaution we took the affected systems offline, which interrupted fleet operations for some customers for up to three days. We engaged outside cybersecurity experts and notified law enforcement. Based on our investigation, we concluded that the incident was not material, and we have not identified evidence that customer data was taken. We incurred $1.8 million of incident response costs, which are included in "other non-recurring items" in our Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation, and we issued $2.3 million of service level credits.

**Updated 2026 outlook.** We now expect full-year 2026 revenue of $470 million to $490 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $70 million to $78 million. This replaces the outlook we provided on March 9, 2026.
