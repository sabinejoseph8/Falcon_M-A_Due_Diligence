## Potential payments upon a change in control

Our executive officers have change-in-control agreements. Cash severance is "double-trigger": it is paid only if the executive is terminated without cause, or resigns for good reason, within 24 months after a change in control. However, all unvested equity awards held by executive officers vest in full immediately upon a change in control ("single-trigger"), whether or not the executive's employment ends.

## Certain relationships and related party transactions

We lease our Columbus, Ohio headquarters and main assembly facility from Hale Family Properties LLC, an entity controlled by Mr. Hale and members of his family. The lease runs through December 2033. Rent paid in 2025 was $2.1 million, with 3% annual increases. The Audit Committee approved the lease when it was renewed in 2023. Under the lease, a change in control of Talonfield allows the landlord to reset the rent to "fair market value", as determined by an appraiser selected by the landlord.
