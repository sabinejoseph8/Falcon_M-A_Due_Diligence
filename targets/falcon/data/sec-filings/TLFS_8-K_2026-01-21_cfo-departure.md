## Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Appointment of Certain Officers

On January 20, 2026, Daniel Okafor notified Talonfield Systems, Inc. of his resignation as Chief Financial Officer, effective February 15, 2026. Mr. Okafor's resignation was not the result of any disagreement with the Company on any matter relating to the Company's operations, policies or practices.

The Board of Directors appointed Rachel Simmons, 41, Vice President and Corporate Controller, as interim Chief Financial Officer, effective February 15, 2026. Ms. Simmons joined the Company in 2021. The Company has begun a search for a permanent Chief Financial Officer.

In connection with his departure, the Company and Mr. Okafor entered into a separation agreement that provides for a cash payment of $900,000 and a general release of claims. Mr. Okafor agreed to provide transition services through March 31, 2026.
