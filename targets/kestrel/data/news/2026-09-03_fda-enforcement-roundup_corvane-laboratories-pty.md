# FDA warning letter to Australian compounder Corvane Laboratories over sterile drug imports

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** FDA Enforcement Roundup (weekly newsletter)
**Date:** September 3, 2026
**Author:** Staff report

The FDA issued a warning letter to **Corvane Laboratories Pty Ltd**, a compounding pharmacy in Brisbane, Australia, citing sterility problems with injectable products it shipped to U.S. clinics. The FDA placed the company's products on import alert.

Corvane Laboratories Pty Ltd is a private Australian company. It is **not affiliated** with Corvane Diagnostics, Inc., the Raleigh, North Carolina, testing laboratory, which confirmed it has no relationship with the Australian firm.
