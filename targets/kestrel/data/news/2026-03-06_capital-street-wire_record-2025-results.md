# Corvane posts record 2025 as OncoTrace drives 11.7% growth

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Capital Street Wire (financial news service)
**Date:** March 6, 2026
**Author:** Lena Hartwick

Corvane Diagnostics (NASDAQ: CRVD) reported record fourth-quarter revenue of US$120.5 million and full-year revenue of US$431.0 million, up 11.7%. Adjusted EBITDA rose 25.4% to US$125.3 million. The company guided to FY2026 revenue of US$455 to 465 million.

CEO Martin Ellery told analysts on the earnings call that "collections have never been stronger." Asked about the jump in fourth-quarter revenue, CFO Renata Solberg said it reflected "strong volumes and a refinement of our collection estimates." She did not give an amount on the call.

Shares rose 6.4% to US$22.30.
