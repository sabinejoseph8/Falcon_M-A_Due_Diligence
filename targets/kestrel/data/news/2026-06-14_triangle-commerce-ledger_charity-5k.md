# Corvane 5K raises record sum for cancer research

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Triangle Commerce Ledger (regional business newspaper)
**Date:** June 14, 2026
**Author:** Keisha Orlando

More than 3,000 runners took part in Saturday's Corvane Hope 5K in downtown Raleigh, raising US$410,000 for a regional cancer center. It was the event's sixth year.

Corvane employees made up about a fifth of the field. The company matched employee fundraising up to US$100,000. Next year's race is set for June 2027.
