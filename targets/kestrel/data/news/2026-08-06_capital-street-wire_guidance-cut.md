# Corvane slashes 2026 outlook; shares plunge 31%

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Capital Street Wire (financial news service)
**Date:** August 6, 2026
**Author:** Lena Hartwick

Corvane Diagnostics cut its FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA outlook to US$100 to 106 million from US$134 to 140 million and its revenue outlook to US$405 to 415 million from US$455 to 465 million. Second-quarter revenue fell 3.0% to US$100.8 million.

Shares fell 31.2% to US$12.45 on Wednesday, from US$18.10 at Tuesday's close, about where they had traded since the end of July.

On the call, analysts asked whether the company would meet the tighter leverage test in its credit agreement at the end of September. CFO Renata Solberg said Corvane "has a constructive relationship with its lenders" and declined to give a leverage figure. Management did not quantify the effect of the new Medicare rate for OncoTrace, saying it would apply only from 2027.
