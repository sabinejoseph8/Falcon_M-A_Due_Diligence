# Corvane's compliance chief departs after three years

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Lab Industry Insider (trade publication)
**Date:** January 15, 2026
**Author:** Paul Maddox

Daniel Pruitt, Chief Compliance Officer of Corvane Diagnostics, has left the company, according to an internal memo seen by Lab Industry Insider. The memo said Mr. Pruitt was leaving "to pursue other opportunities" and that the Deputy General Counsel would serve as interim compliance chief.

Mr. Pruitt joined Corvane in 2023 from a hospital system. Two former Corvane employees, who asked not to be named, said Mr. Pruitt had raised concerns internally in 2025 about how the company's physician speaker program was managed. Lab Industry Insider could not confirm those accounts. Corvane said it "does not comment on personnel matters" and that its compliance program "remains robust."

The compliance officer is not an executive officer, so Corvane was not required to file a Form 8-K about the departure.
