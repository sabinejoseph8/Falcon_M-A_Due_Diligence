# New 25% tariff on imported sequencing reagents squeezes U.S. labs

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Supply Chain Health (trade publication)
**Date:** September 17, 2026
**Author:** Omar Lindqvist

A 25% tariff on imported sequencing reagents and consumables takes effect on October 1, 2026. Analysts estimate that reagents make up 15% to 25% of the cost of a genetic test, so labs that buy at spot prices could see gross margins fall by several points.

Exposure varies. Corvane Diagnostics said its reagent supply agreement with Helixor Instruments fixes prices, including duties, through 2028, and that it expects "no meaningful impact" from the tariff during the contract term. Smaller labs without long-term contracts are most exposed, analysts said.
