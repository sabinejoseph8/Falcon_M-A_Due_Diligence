# Ransomware gang claims Corvane patient data as labs stay offline

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Lab Industry Insider (trade publication)
**Date:** April 22, 2026
**Author:** Paul Maddox

Corvane Diagnostics' laboratories have been unable to process most samples since April 18, after what physicians' offices describe as a ransomware attack. Several oncology practices told Lab Industry Insider they had been asked to send samples to other labs.

A ransomware group posted a message on its leak site on April 21 claiming to have taken "1.1 terabytes" of Corvane files, including patient test results, and set a deadline for payment. Lab Industry Insider has seen screenshots of the posting but could not verify the claim or the files.

Corvane said it was "working around the clock to restore systems" and declined to comment on the group's claim.
