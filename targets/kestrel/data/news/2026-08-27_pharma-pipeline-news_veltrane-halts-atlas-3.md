# Veltrane halts Phase 3 ATLAS-3 trial of VLT-220 for futility, cuts 40% of staff

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Pharma Pipeline News (trade publication)
**Date:** August 27, 2026
**Author:** Dana Kowalczyk

Veltrane Therapeutics said on Thursday that it will stop its ATLAS-3 Phase 3 trial of VLT-220 after an independent data monitoring committee found the drug was unlikely to meet its main goal. Veltrane will close study sites by the first quarter of 2027 and cut about 40% of its workforce.

VLT-220 was Veltrane's lead program. The company said it would focus its remaining cash on two early-stage programs. It did not name its trial vendors, but said it would "wind down vendor contracts in an orderly way."
