# CMS cuts Medicare rate for hereditary cancer panel code by 30% in final 2027 lab fee schedule

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Healthcare Policy Daily (trade publication)
**Date:** July 30, 2026
**Author:** Marcus Ibe

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services on Wednesday published final 2027 payment rates for the clinical laboratory fee schedule. Among the largest changes, the rate for PLA code 0419U, used for Corvane Diagnostics' 84-gene OncoTrace hereditary cancer panel, falls from US$2,150 to **US$1,505**, a cut of 30%. The new rate takes effect on January 1, 2027.

CMS said the new rate reflects a "gapfill" review that compared the panel with similar tests that cost less to run. Rate determinations in the final schedule can be reconsidered only in limited circumstances.

"This is a direct hit for any lab whose volume is concentrated in that code," said a reimbursement consultant. Commercial contracts that are priced as a percentage of the Medicare rate will also reprice, she said.
