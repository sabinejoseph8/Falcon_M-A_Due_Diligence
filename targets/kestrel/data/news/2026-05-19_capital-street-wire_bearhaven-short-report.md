# Short seller Bearhaven targets Corvane, alleging kickbacks and flattered results

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Capital Street Wire (financial news service)
**Date:** May 19, 2026
**Author:** Lena Hartwick

Bearhaven Research, which disclosed a short position in Corvane Diagnostics, published a report on Tuesday alleging that:

1. Corvane paid "speaker fees" averaging US$40,000 a year to dozens of high-ordering oncologists, which Bearhaven says may violate the federal Anti-Kickback Statute;
2. sales staff encouraged physicians to order the full OncoTrace panel when a smaller test was appropriate ("test stacking");
3. fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was "manufactured" by a change in collection estimates even as payer denials rose; and
4. receivables are growing much faster than revenue.

The report cites interviews with eight unnamed former employees and an analysis of public Medicare payment data. Bearhaven says it stands to profit if the share price falls.

Corvane called the report "false and misleading" and said its speaker program "complies with all applicable laws." Shares fell 11.2% to US$17.85.
