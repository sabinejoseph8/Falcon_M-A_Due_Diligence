# Halvard Ridge in exclusive talks to take Corvane private, sources say

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Capital Street Wire (financial news service)
**Date:** October 5, 2026
**Author:** Lena Hartwick

Private equity firm Halvard Ridge Capital Partners is in exclusive talks to buy Corvane Diagnostics for about US$24 a share in cash, according to people familiar with the matter. Corvane's board has formed a special committee of independent directors to consider the approach, one of the people said.

Corvane shares closed at **US$13.10** on Friday, October 2, before the report, and rose 9.2% to US$14.30 in early trading on Monday. The shares traded at US$27.80 a year ago.

A deal is not certain. Halvard and Corvane declined to comment.
