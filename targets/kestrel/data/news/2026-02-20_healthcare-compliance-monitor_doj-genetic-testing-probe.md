# DOJ widens look at genetic test billing; Corvane among labs served, sources say

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Healthcare Compliance Monitor (newsletter)
**Date:** February 20, 2026
**Author:** Staff report

The U.S. Department of Justice has sent civil investigative demands to several genetic testing laboratories about Medicare billing for hereditary cancer panels, according to two people familiar with the matter who were not authorized to speak publicly.

One of the people said Corvane Diagnostics received a demand "in the second week of February" covering billing since 2019 and the company's physician speaker events. Civil investigative demands are often used in False Claims Act investigations, which can begin with a sealed whistleblower ("qui tam") complaint.

Corvane did not respond to requests for comment. A DOJ spokesperson declined to comment. This report is based on anonymous sources and has not been confirmed by the company or the government.
