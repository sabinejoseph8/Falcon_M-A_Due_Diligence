# Corvane opens seventh laboratory in Durham, adds 120 jobs

> FICTIONAL. This article, its publication and everyone in it are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Publication:** Triangle Commerce Ledger (regional business newspaper)
**Date:** November 6, 2025
**Author:** Keisha Orlando

Corvane Diagnostics cut the ribbon on Thursday at a 48,000-square-foot laboratory in Durham's research park. The site will run high-volume molecular tests and is expected to employ 120 people by the end of 2026.

"Durham gives us room to grow OncoTrace volumes for the next five years," said founder and CEO Martin Ellery. The county offered a property tax grant of US$0.6 million over five years.

Local officials praised the project. The company said start-up costs for the new site will be reported outside its Adjusted EBITDA, as it has done for earlier lab openings.
