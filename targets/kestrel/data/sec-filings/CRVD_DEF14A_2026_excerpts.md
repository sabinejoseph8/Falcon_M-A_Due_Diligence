## Certain relationships and related transactions

**Raleigh headquarters laboratory lease.** Corvane leases its Raleigh headquarters and main laboratory from Ellery Property Holdings LLC, which is owned by Mr. Ellery and his spouse. Base rent was US$4.8 million in FY2025, with 3.5% annual increases.

In November 2025, the Audit Committee approved an amendment that extended the lease term from 2030 to **2040**. Before approving the amendment, the Audit Committee obtained an independent appraisal, which estimated market rent for the property at **US$3.6 million** a year. The Audit Committee approved the extension at the existing rent, citing the cost and disruption of relocating a high-complexity laboratory.

There were no other related-party transactions above US$120,000 in FY2025.
