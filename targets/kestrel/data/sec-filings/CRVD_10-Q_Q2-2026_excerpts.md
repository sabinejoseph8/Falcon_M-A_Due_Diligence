Second quarter revenue was US$100.8 million, down 3.0% from US$103.9 million in the second quarter of 2025.

Clinical testing revenue declined because of lower collections on OncoTrace claims and the cybersecurity incident described below. The initial claim denial rate rose to 16.2% in the first half of 2026 (FY2025: 13.4%), mainly because two commercial payers introduced prior authorization for hereditary cancer panels.

### Reimbursement

On July 29, 2026, CMS published final 2027 rates for the clinical laboratory fee schedule, including a new rate for code 0419U (OncoTrace). The new rate takes effect on January 1, 2027. We do not expect it to affect our 2026 results. We are evaluating the effect on future periods and may seek reconsideration.

### Biopharma services

Backlog was US$231.4 million at June 30, 2026 (December 31, 2025: US$246.0 million). Backlog includes **US$61.0 million** related to Veltrane Therapeutics' VLT-220 program. Book-to-bill was 0.7 in the first half of 2026.

### Cybersecurity incident

On April 18, 2026, we detected a ransomware attack on our network. We took systems offline, and testing at our laboratories was disrupted for nine days. In June 2026 we mailed notification letters to approximately **412,300 individuals** whose personal and health information may have been involved. We incurred US$6.1 million of incident response costs in the first half of 2026, which we exclude from Adjusted EBITDA. We estimate lost revenue of about US$4.0 million. Our cyber insurance policy has a limit of US$10.0 million. Several putative class actions have been filed, and the HHS Office for Civil Rights has opened an inquiry. We cannot estimate a range of possible loss.

### Liquidity and capital resources

At June 30, 2026, we had cash of US$28.8 million, US$389.7 million outstanding on our term loan and US$35.0 million drawn on our revolving credit facility, leaving US$65.0 million of revolving capacity. **We were in compliance with the financial covenant in our credit agreement at June 30, 2026.** The maximum Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio steps down from 4.50x to 4.00x at the September 30, 2026 test date.

## Part II, Item 1. Legal Proceedings (excerpts)

**Government investigation.** On February 11, 2026, we received a civil investigative demand (CID) from the U.S. Department of Justice requesting documents about our billing of genetic tests to Medicare, including OncoTrace, and our physician speaker program, for the period from January 2019. We are cooperating with the investigation. We cannot predict its outcome or estimate a range of possible loss, and we have not recorded an accrual.

**Genaro Biosciences v. Corvane.** Claim construction briefing was completed in the second quarter. We continue to believe Genaro's claims are without merit. No accrual has been recorded.

**Hollis v. Corvane.** On June 9, 2026, the court dismissed the case with prejudice.

**Data security litigation.** See "Cybersecurity incident" above.
