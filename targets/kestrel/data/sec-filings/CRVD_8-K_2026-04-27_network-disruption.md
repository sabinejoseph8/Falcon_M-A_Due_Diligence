## Item 8.01. Other Events

On April 18, 2026, Corvane Diagnostics, Inc. identified a network disruption affecting some of its information systems. Corvane promptly took affected systems offline as a precaution, engaged outside cybersecurity experts and notified law enforcement.

Corvane restored laboratory operations on April 26, 2026, and is processing the backlog of patient samples. **Corvane has no evidence at this time that patient information was exfiltrated.** The investigation is ongoing.

Corvane does not currently believe the disruption will have a material impact on its financial condition or results of operations.
