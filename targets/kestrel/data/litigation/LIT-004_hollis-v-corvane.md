# Hollis v. Corvane Diagnostics, Inc.

> FICTIONAL. This case, the parties and the court record are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Court:** Superior Court of North Carolina, Wake County
**Case number:** 24-CVS-011873
**Type:** Employment (putative wage and hour class action)
**Plaintiff:** Jordan Hollis, former phlebotomist, on behalf of about 340 current and former phlebotomists
**Defendant:** Corvane Diagnostics, Inc.

## Claims

Plaintiff alleged that Corvane failed to pay overtime for time spent traveling between patient service centers, and sought about US$2.4 million in back pay, liquidated damages and fees.
