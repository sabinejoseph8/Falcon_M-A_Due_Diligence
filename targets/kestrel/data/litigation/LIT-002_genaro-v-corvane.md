# Genaro Biosciences, Inc. v. Corvane Diagnostics, Inc.

> FICTIONAL. This case, the parties and the court record are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Court:** U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware
**Case number:** 1:25-cv-00412
**Type:** Patent infringement
**Patent:** U.S. Patent No. 11,604,318 ("the '318 patent"), "Methods for target capture in multi-gene sequencing panels"
**Accused product:** OncoTrace hereditary cancer panel (current version)

## Claims

Genaro alleges that the target-capture step used in OncoTrace infringes claims 1, 4 and 9 of the '318 patent. Genaro seeks damages and a permanent injunction against further sales of the accused version of OncoTrace.
