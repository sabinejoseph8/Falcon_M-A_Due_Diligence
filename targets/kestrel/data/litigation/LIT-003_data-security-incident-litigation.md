# In re Corvane Diagnostics Data Security Incident Litigation

> FICTIONAL. This case, the parties and the court record are invented for a portfolio demonstration.

**Court:** U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina
**Case number:** 5:26-cv-00341 (lead case)
**Type:** Consumer class action (data breach)
**Plaintiffs:** Patients whose information was involved in the April 2026 incident, on behalf of a putative nationwide class
**Defendant:** Corvane Diagnostics, Inc.

## Allegations in the consolidated complaint (filed September 28, 2026)

The following are plaintiffs' allegations and have not been proven:

- On April 18, 2026, a ransomware group gained access to Corvane's network and took about 1.1 terabytes of data, including names, dates of birth, insurance information and genetic test results.
- Corvane's outside forensic firm gave Corvane **preliminary findings of data exfiltration on April 24, 2026**. Plaintiffs cite an internal timeline produced in early discovery.
- On April 27, 2026, Corvane told investors in a Form 8-K that it had "no evidence at this time that patient information was exfiltrated."
- Corvane did not mail notification letters until **June 12, 2026**, to approximately **412,300 individuals**, about eight weeks after the attack.
- Corvane failed to use multi-factor authentication on a remote-access account that the attackers used.

Plaintiffs bring claims for negligence, breach of implied contract and violations of state consumer protection and genetic privacy laws. They seek damages, statutory damages under state genetic privacy laws, credit and identity monitoring, and an order requiring security improvements.
