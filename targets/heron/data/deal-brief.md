## 1. The proposed transaction

Meridale Capital Partners Fund IV ("Meridale") proposes to take private **Delmora Home Systems, Inc.** (NASDAQ: DLHS, "Delmora"), a US maker of connected home climate devices, through a one-step cash merger.

The Delmora board formed a Strategic Review Committee in March 2026 and ran a limited sale process. Meridale is the only remaining bidder.

## 2. About Delmora

Delmora designs and sells smart thermostats, air quality monitors and hub devices under the Delmora brand. Products are assembled mainly in Monterrey, Mexico, and sold through retailers, online marketplaces, utilities and international distributors. FY2025 revenue was US$786.0 million in three segments:

- **Devices** (US$503.0 million): thermostats, sensors and hubs. Products run Delmora's **AdaptiveComfort** learning algorithm.
- **Subscriptions & Services** (US$142.0 million): the Delmora Care subscription and, since September 2025, **Delmora Insights**, which licenses aggregated home energy data to partners.
- **Utility Programs** (US$141.0 million): demand-response and rebate programs run for public utility agencies and utilities.

Delmora was founded in 2018 by Dana Whitcombe (CEO) and Dr. Anselm Reyes-Barrow (CTO). In 2019 it acquired Pittock Heating, an Ohio heating equipment maker, mainly for its utility relationships. In 2024 it acquired Nimbus Air Analytics, a data analytics start-up that became the base of Delmora Insights.

## 3. Investment thesis (deal team view)

1. **Recurring revenue growth.** Subscriptions & Services grew 17.4% in FY2025. Delmora Insights is the main growth engine: the model assumes Insights revenue rises to US$60.0 million by 2028. The deal team attributes about **US$310 million of enterprise value** to Insights.
2. **Platform licensing.** License AdaptiveComfort to heating and appliance makers (OEMs) from 2028.
3. **Stable utility base.** Utility Programs renews and grows 6% a year. The largest program, with the Western States Energy Alliance, is assumed to renew at the end of 2026 on similar terms.
4. **International expansion.** Distributor sales outside North America and Europe rose sharply in FY2025, led by the Middle East.
5. **Cash conversion.** Operating cash flow was US$134.1 million in H1 2026, more than the whole of FY2025. Leverage after closing is about 6.9x LTM Adjusted EBITDA and falls quickly.

## 5. Questions for the Investment Committee

The Investment Committee asks the diligence team to answer six questions:

1. **Quality of earnings and cash flow:** Is the LTM Adjusted EBITDA of US$130.4 million and the H1 2026 operating cash flow a sound base for the price and the debt package? What adjustments are needed?
2. **Inherited liabilities:** What contingent, off-balance-sheet and change-of-control liabilities would Meridale inherit, and how large are they compared with the price?
3. **Technology and data rights:** Does Delmora own its core technology and have the right to use customer data in the way the growth plan (Insights and OEM licensing) assumes?
4. **Regulatory, trade and legal exposure:** Which regulatory, trade, product safety and legal matters could delay or block closing, or impair the business after closing?
5. **Revenue durability:** How durable are the main revenue streams (utility programs, subscriptions, distributors) over the hold period?
6. **Price and protections:** Is US$24.50 per share supportable? What price changes, structure, conditions and protections should Meridale seek?
