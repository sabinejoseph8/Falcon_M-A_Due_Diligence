# Family says smart thermostat started kitchen wall fire

**WCOV Columbus News (local television website)** | January 14, 2026

A Westerville family says a smart thermostat set fire to their kitchen wall on New Year's Eve. Nobody was hurt. Firefighters put out the fire within minutes, but smoke damage forced the family out of the house for two weeks.

The Westerville fire marshal's report, seen by WCOV, says the fire started "at or near the wall-mounted thermostat". The device was a Delmora Aura, model AT-210, bought in 2022. The homeowner said the thermostat was wired to a 1990s gas furnace.

"The screen went black, then we smelled burning plastic," the homeowner said. "By the time I got there the wall was on fire."

WCOV found more than 40 posts on a Delmora user forum since 2024 describing Aura thermostats that became hot, swelled or gave off smoke. Several posters mentioned older furnaces.

In a statement, Delmora Home Systems said it was "aware of a small number of reports" and was "investigating with an independent engineering firm". The company said customer safety was its top priority.
