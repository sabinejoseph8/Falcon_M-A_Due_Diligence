# Delmora beats Q4 estimates as Insights launch impresses analysts

**Smart Home Insider (trade newsletter)** | February 27, 2026

Delmora Home Systems (DLHS) reported fourth-quarter revenue of $217.0 million, up 6.9% from a year earlier and ahead of analyst estimates. Full-year revenue was $786.0 million. Shares rose 8% in early trading.

Chief executive Dana Whitcombe spent much of the call on Delmora Insights, the data product launched in September. "We already have 2.1 million households enrolled, and our partners in insurance and real estate are asking for more," Whitcombe said. Two analysts called Insights "the most underappreciated asset in smart home".

Gross margin reached 42.5%, a record, helped by a weaker Mexican peso and lower warranty costs. Chief financial officer Martin Okafor said the company would "keep a disciplined approach to currency" after ending its hedging program last year.

Delmora guided to FY2026 revenue of $830 million to $850 million.

*Our view:* a solid quarter. Watch Insights enrollment and the WSEA re-bid later this year.
