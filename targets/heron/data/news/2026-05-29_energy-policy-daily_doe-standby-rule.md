# DOE finalizes standby power limits for smart thermostats

**Energy Policy Daily** | May 29, 2026

The US Department of Energy on Thursday finalized a rule limiting the "connected standby" power that smart thermostats may draw when idle. The limits take effect in January 2028.

Analysts said older devices with always-on cellular modules may need redesign. A note from one brokerage listed several thermostat makers, including Delmora Home Systems, as "potentially exposed", without giving details. The DOE estimates industry compliance costs of $40 million to $60 million in total.

An industry group said most current Wi-Fi models already meet the limits. Delmora did not respond to a request for comment by publication time.
