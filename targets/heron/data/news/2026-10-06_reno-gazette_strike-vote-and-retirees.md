# Delmora warehouse workers authorize strike; Ohio retirees eye sale

**Reno Gazette** | October 6, 2026

Workers at Delmora Home Systems' Reno distribution center voted 91% in favor of authorizing a strike, Distribution Workers United Local 88 said on Monday. The 410 workers have been working under a day-to-day extension since their contract expired on September 30.

The union is seeking wage increases of 6% a year and limits on mandatory overtime during the holiday peak. "We've moved record volumes for this company and we want our share," a union steward said. A Delmora spokesperson said the company was "bargaining in good faith" and had contingency plans to keep products moving.

The union also asked any buyer of Delmora to commit to recognizing the contract, which includes a successorship clause.

Separately, a group representing retirees of Pittock Heating, the Ohio company Delmora bought in 2019, said it was following the sale talks closely. The group's chair said a 2019 trust agreement requires Delmora to "fully fund the pension on a buyout basis" within 120 days of any change of control. "By the plan actuary's numbers that is well over $200 million," the chair said. "We expect every dollar."
