# WSEA picks Thermiq Energy for 2027 to 2031 smart thermostat program

**Utility Programs West (trade publication)** | July 22, 2026

The board of the Western States Energy Alliance (WSEA), a public joint-powers agency serving 14 municipal utilities, voted on Tuesday to award its 2027 to 2031 Smart Thermostat and Demand Response Program to Thermiq Energy.

The five-year contract is worth an estimated $510 million. It replaces WSEA's current program, run by Delmora Home Systems since 2019, which ends on December 31, 2026.

According to the evaluation summary published with the board materials, Thermiq's proposal scored highest of four bidders on price and on a new requirement for open device standards. Delmora's proposal ranked third. WSEA's procurement rules give unsuccessful bidders until August 4 to file a protest.

A WSEA spokesperson said existing Delmora thermostats in customers' homes would be "transitioned to the new program where technically feasible". Delmora said it was "reviewing the decision".
