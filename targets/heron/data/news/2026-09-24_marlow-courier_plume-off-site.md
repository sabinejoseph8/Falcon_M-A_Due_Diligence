# Chemical found in groundwater under Linden Park homes near old Pittock plant

**The Marlow Courier (Marlow, Ohio)** | September 24, 2026

Trichloroethylene (TCE), an industrial solvent, has been found in groundwater under the Linden Park neighborhood, about 0.4 miles from the former Pittock Heating plant, the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency said on Tuesday.

Samples from two new monitoring wells in Linden Park showed TCE above the federal drinking water limit. Homes in the area use city water, but the Ohio EPA has directed Pittock, now owned by Delmora Home Systems, to test indoor air in about 60 homes for vapor intrusion and to submit a plan to investigate the full extent of the plume by December 15.

"This is the first confirmation that contamination has left the site," an Ohio EPA spokesperson said.

Several Linden Park residents filed a lawsuit last week against Delmora and Pittock, seeking medical monitoring and compensation for loss of property value. An environmental engineer advising the residents said that off-site investigation and treatment "could cost tens of millions of dollars".

Delmora said it was "working cooperatively with the Ohio EPA".
