# Ensamble Norte misses loan payment after strike

**Monterrey Economia (business daily, translated from Spanish)** | September 15, 2026

Ensamble Norte, the Monterrey electronics assembler jointly owned by Grupo Industrial Arceo and US thermostat maker Delmora Home Systems, did not make a scheduled loan payment of 98 million pesos (about $5.7 million) due on August 31, according to two bankers with knowledge of the matter.

The payment was due under a syndicated term loan from four Mexican banks. The bankers said the lenders sent a reservation of rights letter and gave the company until September 30 to pay. "The banks are looking at the guarantor," one said.

A strike by about 1,100 workers stopped production at Ensamble Norte's plant from August 3 to August 24. The union won a 14% wage increase.

Suppliers said Ensamble Norte's cash position had already tightened in the spring, after its largest customer moved to 120-day payment terms in April. Grupo Arceo said it was "in constructive discussions with the lenders" and that production had returned to normal.
