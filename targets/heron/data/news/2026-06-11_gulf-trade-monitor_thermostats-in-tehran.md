# US smart thermostats turn up in Tehran towers, former distributor staff say

**Gulf Trade Monitor (online newsletter)** | June 11, 2026

Thousands of US-made smart thermostats and home hubs sold to a Dubai distributor have been shipped on to buyers in Iran, according to two former employees of the distributor who spoke on condition of anonymity.

The former employees said Gulf Horizon Trading FZE, which became the Middle East distributor for Delmora Home Systems in 2024, moved Delmora Aura Pro thermostats and Delmora Hub devices through a company in the Sharjah free zone to buyers in Iran from early 2025. One said that "most of the volume" in 2025 went that way. Gulf Trade Monitor could not independently verify the claims. Photos shared by one source, which appear to show Delmora devices installed in a residential tower in north Tehran, could not be dated or located with certainty.

US sanctions generally prohibit the export of US goods to Iran, including through third countries. Delmora devices include cellular modules and encryption functions.

Gulf Horizon said in a statement that it "complies with all applicable laws" and that the claims came from "disgruntled former staff". Delmora Home Systems said it "maintains robust trade compliance controls" and "takes any allegation seriously".
