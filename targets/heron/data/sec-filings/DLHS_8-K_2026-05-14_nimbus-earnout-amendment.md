## Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On May 12, 2026, Delmora Home Systems, Inc. (the "Company") entered into Amendment No. 2 (the "Amendment") to the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated March 1, 2024 among the Company, Nimbus Air Analytics, Inc. and the representative of the former Nimbus securityholders (the "Merger Agreement").

The Merger Agreement provides for contingent consideration (the "Earn-out Payment") of up to US$90.0 million, based on annual recurring revenue of the Company's Delmora Insights product at December 31, 2027.

The Amendment makes the following principal changes:

1. **Acceleration on a change of control.** If a Change of Control of the Company (as defined in the Merger Agreement) occurs before the Earn-out Payment date, the Earn-out Payment will become due **in full at the maximum amount of US$90.0 million**, regardless of Insights annual recurring revenue, and will be payable in cash within ten business days after the Change of Control closes. Before the Amendment, a Change of Control had no effect on the amount or timing of the Earn-out Payment.
2. **Restrictive covenants.** Former Nimbus securityholders who are Company employees agreed to extend their non-competition and non-solicitation covenants from April 2027 to April 2029.
3. **Release.** The securityholder representative released certain claims relating to the Company's integration of the Nimbus business.

The Amendment was approved by the Company's board of directors on May 12, 2026. Leona Farkas, the Company's Chief Data Officer and a former Nimbus securityholder, did not take part in the board's discussion or vote. Leona Farkas held about 34% of Nimbus's outstanding securities before the acquisition.

The foregoing description is qualified by reference to the full text of the Amendment, which will be filed as an exhibit to the Company's Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ending June 30, 2026.
