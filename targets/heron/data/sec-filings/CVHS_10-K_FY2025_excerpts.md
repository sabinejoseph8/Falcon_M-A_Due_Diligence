## PART I

### Item 1. Business

#### Overview

Corvane Home Systems, Inc. ("Corvane", "we", "us") designs, sells and supports connected home climate products: smart thermostats (the Corvane Aura and Corvane Aura Pro lines), indoor air quality monitors, room sensors and the Corvane Hub. At December 31, 2025, about 6.7 million Corvane devices were connected to our cloud platform.

We report three segments:

| Segment | FY2025 revenue (US$ millions) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Devices | 503.0 | Hardware sold through retailers, online marketplaces, utilities and distributors |
| Subscriptions & Services | 142.0 | Corvane Care subscription (remote diagnostics, filter delivery, extended warranty) and Corvane Insights |
| Utility Programs | 141.0 | Demand-response, rebate and energy efficiency programs run for public utility agencies and utilities |

#### AdaptiveComfort technology

All Corvane thermostats run **AdaptiveComfort**, our learning algorithm that builds a thermal model of each home and adjusts heating and cooling schedules. AdaptiveComfort was conceived by our co-founder and Chief Technology Officer, Dr. Anselm Reyes-Barrow, and has been refined by our engineering team since our founding in 2018.

AdaptiveComfort is protected by a family of six issued US patents and corresponding foreign patents. The earliest application in the family, US provisional application No. 62/467,318, was filed on **March 6, 2017**. We believe we own all intellectual property that is material to our business.

In 2019 we transferred the non-US rights to the AdaptiveComfort patent family to our subsidiary Corvane Technologies Ireland Limited under an intellectual property purchase agreement for US$64.0 million, and entered into a cost-sharing arrangement for further development.

#### Corvane Insights

In September 2025 we launched **Corvane Insights**, built on the analytics platform of Nimbus Air Analytics, which we acquired in April 2024. Insights provides utility, insurance and real-estate partners with aggregated and de-identified information about home energy use, heating and cooling patterns and home occupancy patterns. Households with a Corvane Care subscription are enrolled in Insights when they accept the updated Corvane Care terms of service. Customers may opt out at any time in the "Data & Privacy" section of the Corvane app. At December 31, 2025, 2.1 million households were enrolled. Insights revenue was US$3.1 million in FY2025.

#### Manufacturing and suppliers

Most of our devices are assembled by **Ensamble Norte S. de R.L. de C.V.** ("Ensamble Norte") in Monterrey, Mexico. We own 49% of Ensamble Norte; Grupo Industrial Arceo owns 51%. We account for Ensamble Norte under the equity method. In FY2025 we purchased US$212.0 million of products from Ensamble Norte. A large part of our cost of revenue is denominated in Mexican pesos.

#### Halvorsen Heating

In 2019 we acquired Halvorsen Heating LLC ("Halvorsen"), a heating equipment maker based in Marlow, Ohio, mainly for its long-standing utility relationships. We closed Halvorsen's Marlow manufacturing plant in 2020. Halvorsen sponsors a defined benefit pension plan that we assumed (see Note 11).

#### Regulation

Our products must meet federal and state energy efficiency standards. In May 2026 the US Department of Energy is expected to finalize a rule setting connected standby power limits for smart thermostats. All current Corvane thermostat models, including legacy Gen 3 models still in the channel, were tested by an accredited laboratory in 2025 and meet the proposed limits.

In 2022 we entered into a consent order with the Federal Trade Commission (the "2022 FTC Order") resolving an investigation into the disclosure of thermostat usage data to a third-party advertising partner in 2020. See Item 1A.

#### Human capital

At December 31, 2025, we had 2,110 employees. About 405 employees at our Reno, Nevada distribution center are covered by a collective bargaining agreement with Distribution Workers United, Local 88, which expires on September 30, 2026. We believe our employee relations are good.

### Item 1A. Risk Factors (selected)

**We are subject to the 2022 FTC Order, and a violation could result in significant civil penalties.** The 2022 FTC Order, which runs for 20 years, prohibits us from misrepresenting our data practices and requires us to (i) obtain a consumer's **affirmative express consent** before sharing "covered information" with any third party in a way that goes beyond the purposes disclosed when the data was collected, (ii) maintain a comprehensive privacy program, and (iii) obtain biennial privacy assessments. "Covered information" includes thermostat set-point, schedule and occupancy-sensor data linked or reasonably linkable to a household. Violations of an FTC order can result in civil penalties for each violation.

**Our international sales are subject to export control and economic sanctions laws.** We sell through distributors in Europe, Canada and, since 2024, the Middle East. Our devices include cellular and Wi-Fi modules and encryption functions. If a distributor resold our products to a sanctioned country or party, we could face penalties, loss of export privileges and reputational harm. We are not aware of any violations of export control or sanctions laws.

**Our products include open source software.** Our device firmware and hub software include open source components, some licensed under "copyleft" licenses such as the GNU General Public License. If we failed to comply with those licenses we could be required to release proprietary source code, re-engineer products or stop shipments. We believe we comply with the licenses that apply to us.

**Product defects could lead to recalls, claims and higher warranty costs.** Our devices contain lithium batteries and connect to home heating and cooling systems. Defects could cause property damage or injury and lead to recalls, product liability claims or regulatory action by the Consumer Product Safety Commission ("CPSC").

**We depend on a small number of utility program contracts.** Utility Programs revenue is concentrated in a few contracts with public agencies that are awarded through competitive procurement. Our largest, with the Western States Energy Alliance ("WSEA"), runs through December 31, 2026.

**Labor disruption could affect distribution.** A strike or work stoppage at our Reno distribution center could delay shipments.

### Item 3. Legal Proceedings

- **European Commission inspection.** On November 18 and 19, 2025, officials of the European Commission's Directorate-General for Competition carried out unannounced inspections at the Rotterdam offices of Corvane Europe B.V. and at two of our European distributors, concerning possible restrictions on cross-border online sales of thermostats in the European Economic Area. We are cooperating. It is too early to assess the outcome.

- **Brennan Sales Group.** In 2025 a former independent sales representative, Brennan Sales Group LLC, sued us for US$3.2 million in allegedly unpaid commissions. The court dismissed the case with prejudice on January 22, 2026.
- **Aura thermostat battery reports.** We have received a limited number of consumer reports concerning the backup battery in certain Corvane Aura thermostats made between 2021 and 2023. We are investigating these reports with an independent engineering firm. We do not believe this matter is material.

We are involved in other ordinary-course claims that we do not expect to have a material effect.

## PART II

### Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (selected)

#### FY2025 overview

Revenue grew 6.5% to US$786.0 million. Gross margin improved to 42.5% from 41.8%, helped by a weaker Mexican peso (average 19.4 pesos per US dollar in FY2025 against 18.3 in FY2024) and **lower warranty expense**. Adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP measure) was US$133.7 million.

#### Warranty

Warranty expense was US$9.6 million in FY2025, compared with US$15.0 million in FY2024. **In the fourth quarter of 2025 we reduced the accrual rate applied to new device sales, based on favorable claims experience on our Gen 4 products.** The accrued warranty liability was US$12.2 million at December 31, 2025, compared with US$15.1 million a year earlier.

#### Foreign currency

In the third quarter of 2025, given the favorable outlook for the US dollar against the Mexican peso, **we discontinued our program of hedging forecast peso-denominated costs** with forward contracts. At December 31, 2025 we had no foreign currency hedges outstanding. A 10% strengthening of the peso against the US dollar would increase our annual cost of revenue by about US$28 million.

#### International

Revenue from distributors outside North America and Europe rose to US$41.2 million from US$6.0 million, mainly from Gulf Horizon Trading FZE, our distributor in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, appointed in 2024.

#### Liquidity

At December 31, 2025 we had cash of US$59.4 million, long-term debt of US$440.0 million under our term loan maturing in **June 2029**, and an undrawn US$150.0 million revolving credit facility. We were in compliance with all debt covenants with substantial headroom. Operating cash flow was US$86.6 million.

#### FY2023 restructuring

In FY2023 we consolidated three regional warehouses into our Reno distribution center and recorded restructuring costs of US$6.8 million. The program was completed in December 2023 and no further costs are expected.

#### Note 13. Business combinations and contingent consideration

On April 2, 2024 we acquired Nimbus Air Analytics, Inc. ("Nimbus") for US$98.0 million in cash plus contingent consideration (an "earn-out") of up to **US$90.0 million**. The earn-out is payable in 2028 if annual recurring revenue of Corvane Insights reaches US$40.0 million at December 31, 2027, with a reduced amount between US$25.0 million and US$40.0 million and nothing below US$25.0 million. Nimbus's former shareholders include Leona Farkas, now our Chief Data Officer.

We measure the earn-out at fair value each quarter using a Monte Carlo model. Fair value was US$14.5 million at December 31, 2024 and **US$8.0 million at December 31, 2025**. The US$6.5 million decrease, reflecting a slower expected ramp of Insights, was recorded as a gain in other income.

#### Note 14. Commitments and guarantees

**Ensamble Norte guarantee.** We have guaranteed **US$64.0 million** of a term loan owed by Ensamble Norte to a group of Mexican banks. The loan matures in 2028. If Ensamble Norte defaults, the lenders may demand payment from us. We recorded the fair value of the guarantee, US$0.6 million, in other long-term liabilities. Our maximum potential payment is US$64.0 million plus accrued interest and costs. Ensamble Norte has paid its debts as they fall due.

**Purchase commitments.** We had non-cancellable purchase commitments of US$118.0 million at December 31, 2025, mainly with Ensamble Norte.

#### Note 15. Environmental matters

Under the 2021 Ohio EPA consent order, Halvorsen is investigating and remediating TCE contamination in soil and groundwater at the former Marlow plant. The investigation to date has found contamination within the site boundary. Our estimate of remaining costs ranges from **US$9.0 million to US$71.0 million**, depending mainly on whether groundwater contamination has moved beyond the site boundary, which would require off-site investigation and treatment. Because no amount in the range is a better estimate than any other, **we have accrued the low end of the range, US$9.0 million.**
