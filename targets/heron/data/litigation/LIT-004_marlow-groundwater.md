## Claims

Negligence; private nuisance; trespass; medical monitoring.

## Main allegations (allegations only)

- TCE from the former Pittock plant has moved in groundwater beneath Linden Park, about 0.4 miles from the site.
- Pittock's own 2023 site investigation report recommended installing off-site monitoring wells. The complaint alleges they were not installed until the Ohio EPA required them in 2026.

## Related regulatory action (Ohio EPA)

The complaint attaches an Ohio EPA letter to Pittock dated September 22, 2026, under the 2021 administrative consent order. The letter:

- confirms TCE above the federal maximum contaminant level in two off-site monitoring wells in Linden Park;
- directs Pittock to complete vapor intrusion sampling at about 60 homes by November 30, 2026; and
- requires a work plan to delineate the full extent of the off-site plume by December 15, 2026, and states that the agency "will evaluate whether off-site groundwater remedies are required".
