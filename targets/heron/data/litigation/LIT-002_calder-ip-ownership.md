## Claims

1. Declaratory judgment that Calder owns the inventions claimed in US Patent Nos. 10,412,877; 10,598,204; 10,771,365; 11,036,912; 11,284,550 and 11,602,198 (the "AdaptiveComfort patents") and the foreign counterparts.
2. Breach of contract against Dr. Reyes-Barrow.
3. Order requiring assignment of the patents to Calder.
4. Accounting and damages, including a reasonable royalty on Corvane's thermostat sales since 2019.

## Key facts alleged by Calder (allegations, supported by exhibits)

- On September 2, 2014, Dr. Reyes-Barrow signed Calder's Intellectual Property and Invention Assignment Agreement (Exhibit A). It states that the researcher "**hereby assigns** to the Institute all right, title and interest" in inventions "conceived or first reduced to practice" in the course of Institute research or with significant use of Institute resources.
- Dr. Reyes-Barrow was a postdoctoral fellow at Calder's Building Energy Laboratory until June 30, 2017, funded in part by a US Department of Energy research grant.
- A laboratory notebook entry dated February 14, 2017 (Exhibit C) describes an "adaptive thermal envelope model that learns from set-point overrides", which Calder says matches claim 1 of the first AdaptiveComfort patent.
- On March 2, 2017, Dr. Reyes-Barrow presented the model at an internal laboratory seminar (Exhibit D, slides).
- **On March 6, 2017**, while still employed by Calder, Dr. Reyes-Barrow personally filed US provisional application No. 62/467,318, the earliest application in the AdaptiveComfort family. The application was assigned to Corvane in 2018.

## Relief sought

Ownership and assignment of the patents; a royalty of 4% of net sales of Corvane thermostats from 2019; costs. Calder states that, if it obtains ownership, it intends to license the patents "on a non-exclusive basis to qualified manufacturers".

## Defendants' position

Corvane says the invention was conceived after Dr. Reyes-Barrow left Calder, that the February 2017 notebook entry describes a different, academic model, and that the claims are barred by the statute of limitations.
