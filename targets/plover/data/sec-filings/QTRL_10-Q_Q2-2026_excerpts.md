**Change in estimate.** In the second quarter of 2026 we refined our method for estimating the excess and obsolete reserve. The reserve is now based on product-level sell-through forecasts, which use improved sell-through data from our Borealis channel, instead of fixed percentages by age. This change in estimate reduced the reserve and cost of revenue by $15.4 million in Q2 2026 (about $0.22 per diluted share). Inventory more than 12 months old was $109.0 million at cost at June 30, 2026.

### Note 9. Commitments and contingencies

**FTC litigation.** On June 18, 2026 the Federal Trade Commission filed a complaint against us in the U.S. District Court for the District of Oregon about environmental marketing claims for our EverTrail products. The complaint seeks a permanent injunction and monetary relief for consumers. The complaint does not specify an amount. We believe our claims were substantiated and we intend to defend ourselves vigorously. We cannot estimate a range of possible loss and have not recorded an accrual.

**Fennimore arbitration.** On May 11, 2026 Fennimore Heritage LLC filed an arbitration demand with the American Arbitration Association. Fennimore claims that we underpaid royalties under the Alder & Pine licence by $11.6 million, plus interest, for 2021 to 2025, by deducting co-operative marketing fees, chargebacks and markdown allowances from Net Sales. Fennimore also seeks a declaration that the underpayment is a material breach. We believe our royalty calculations are consistent with industry practice. A hearing is scheduled for March 2027.

**Dealer litigation.** On April 2, 2026 the court denied our motion to dismiss the claims brought by Summit & Spoke Outfitters LLC and 37 other former dealers. The plaintiffs seek damages of $33.5 million and reinstatement. Discovery is under way.

**Internal investigation.** In March 2026 our internal audit function identified payments of about $2.9 million made from 2021 to 2025 to Meridian Trade Facilitation Co., a customs broker, recorded as "expedited processing fees," for which supporting documentation was inadequate. The Audit Committee engaged outside counsel to investigate. In May 2026 we voluntarily disclosed the matter to the U.S. Department of Justice and the Securities and Exchange Commission under the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act. We stopped using Meridian for new shipments from July 1, 2026 and are moving that volume to other brokers. We cannot predict the outcome. Possible consequences include fines, disgorgement and the appointment of a compliance monitor. Investigation costs were $4.2 million in H1 2026.

**Unclaimed property audit.** In April 2026 the Delaware Department of Finance notified Quillmark Gift Card LLC of an unclaimed property audit covering 2011 to 2025. Where records are incomplete, Delaware may estimate amounts owed. Based on a preliminary analysis, we believe a loss is reasonably possible in a range of $9 million to $31 million, including interest. We have not recorded an accrual.

**Cedar Hollow permit.** In March 2026 we applied to renew Conditional Use Permit CUP-2016-044 for our Cedar Hollow distribution center, which expires on December 31, 2026. A neighborhood group has opposed the renewal. We expect a decision in the fourth quarter of 2026.

**Down certification.** In March 2026 the Responsible Down Standard certificate of one of our down suppliers was suspended for a documentation lapse. It was reinstated in May 2026 after a re-audit. We did not change any product claims.

## Part II, Item 1A. Risk factors (update)

We continue to work toward our goal of moving all products to PFAS-free finishes by the end of FY2028, and we are evaluating how these laws apply to our products and inventory.
