### Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

**Merger Agreement.** On September 14, 2026, Quillmark Trail Co. (the "Company") entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the "Merger Agreement") with Ridgeline Brands Group, Inc. ("Ridgeline") and a wholly owned subsidiary of Ridgeline. The board of directors, acting on the unanimous recommendation of a special committee of independent directors, approved the Merger Agreement.

Key terms:

- **Offer.** Ridgeline will commence a cash tender offer for all outstanding Class A and Class B shares at $24.00 per share. The offer is scheduled to expire at one minute after 11:59 p.m. Eastern on November 13, 2026, unless extended.
- **Conditions.** The offer is conditioned on, among other things, the tender of shares representing a majority of the voting power of the outstanding shares, expiration of the HSR Act waiting period, and the absence of any order restraining the offer. The offer is not conditioned on financing or on any third-party consent.
- **Go-shop.** Until 11:59 p.m. Eastern on November 13, 2026, the Company may solicit, and negotiate, alternative acquisition proposals.
- **Termination fees.** If the Company terminates the Merger Agreement to accept a superior proposal from a party that submitted a proposal during the go-shop period, it will pay Ridgeline $18.7 million. In other specified circumstances it will pay $43.7 million.
- **Equity awards.** All unvested restricted stock units (about 1.6 million) will vest in full at the effective time and be paid out at the offer price.

**Tender and Support Agreement.** Marisol Achterberg, the Company's Founder and CEO, who holds all 10.4 million Class B shares and 0.6 million Class A shares (about 71.8% of the total voting power), has agreed to tender her shares into the offer. The support agreement terminates if the Merger Agreement is terminated.

**Consulting Agreement.** In connection with the Merger Agreement, Ms. Achterberg entered into a Consulting and Restrictive Covenant Agreement with Ridgeline, effective at closing. Under it:

- Ms. Achterberg will resign as Chief Executive Officer, Chief Creative Officer and director of the Company at closing and become "Founder Advisor" to Ridgeline.
- She will provide advisory services of up to 10 hours a month for four years.
- Ridgeline will pay her a consulting fee of $8.0 million a year for four years ($32.0 million in total), payable whether or not services are requested.
- She agrees not to compete with Ridgeline or the Company for four years.

The special committee was informed of the Consulting Agreement before approving the Merger Agreement.
