## Part I, Item 1. Business

### Overview

Quillmark designs and sells outdoor apparel, footwear and accessories under the Quillmark brand and, under licence, the Alder & Pine brand. We were founded in 2009 by Marisol Achterberg, who serves as our Chief Executive Officer and Chief Creative Officer.

**Borealis Market.** Borealis Market Inc. ("Borealis") operates the largest US online marketplace for outdoor goods. We sell to Borealis under a vendor agreement. Borealis purchases our products at wholesale prices, takes title on delivery to its fulfillment centers, and sets retail prices. Under the agreement:

- Borealis may return unsold products for credit, up to 20% of its purchases from us in the two most recent fiscal quarters (the "stock rotation right").
- We pay Borealis a co-operative marketing fee equal to 6.0% of its gross purchases from us (5.8% in FY2024). Co-op fees were $14.2 million in FY2025 and $11.4 million in FY2024.
- Borealis committed to minimum annual purchases of $150.0 million.
- The current agreement expires on December 31, 2026.

**Specialty dealers.** In March 2025 we ended our Authorized Specialty Dealer agreements with 141 smaller dealers, whose purchases from us totaled $29.0 million in FY2024, to focus on larger wholesale accounts and digital channels. See Item 3.

### The Alder & Pine licence

We sell Alder & Pine products under an Amended and Restated License Agreement with Fennimore Heritage LLC ("Fennimore") dated March 1, 2019 (Exhibit 10.4). Key terms:

- **Scope:** exclusive worldwide licence for apparel, footwear and accessories.
- **Term:** to December 31, 2029. Renewal for a further five years requires mutual agreement.
- **Royalty:** 8.0% of Net Sales, with a minimum annual royalty of $10.0 million. Royalty expense was $13.1 million in FY2025 and $12.0 million in FY2024 and is included in cost of revenue.
- **Net Sales (Section 1.12):** gross invoiced sales less returns actually credited, trade discounts shown on the invoice, and sales taxes. "No deduction shall be made for advertising, co-operative marketing, promotional allowances, chargebacks, uncollectible accounts or costs of collection."
- **Key person (Section 14.3):** Fennimore may terminate the agreement on 90 days' written notice if Marisol Achterberg ceases, for any reason other than death or permanent disability, to serve as Chief Executive Officer or Chief Creative Officer of the licensee, unless Fennimore has approved her successor in writing.
- **Breach (Section 14.1):** either party may terminate for a material breach that is not cured within 60 days of notice.
- **Change of control (Section 15.2):** the licence may be assigned in connection with a merger or sale of the licensee without Fennimore's consent, provided the licensee remains bound by all terms.

### Sourcing and distribution

Our products are made by independent contract manufacturers, mainly in Asia. For footwear, customs clearance at Port Selang is handled by Meridian Trade Facilitation Co. ("Meridian"), a licensed customs broker. Meridian cleared 41% of our footwear import volume in FY2025.

We have a supply agreement with our main nylon fabric mill that fixes prices through December 31, 2027 for about 70% of our expected nylon volume.

Our Cedar Hollow distribution center in Linwood County, Oregon, shipped 72% of our units in FY2025. During peak season (October to December) it operates 24 hours a day under Linwood County Conditional Use Permit CUP-2016-044, which expires on December 31, 2026. We expect to apply for renewal in 2026.

### Materials and product claims

About 31% of our apparel units in FY2025 used a durable water repellent ("DWR") finish containing fluorinated chemistry (PFAS). We plan to move all products to PFAS-free finishes by the end of FY2028. Several states have adopted or proposed restrictions on PFAS in textiles. We monitor these developments and believe our transition plan addresses them.

Our EverTrail product line, launched in 2022, is marketed as "Carbon-Neutral" and "Made with 100% Recycled Nylon." We support the carbon-neutral claim by retiring carbon credits purchased from the Rio Verde Forest Carbon Project. In January 2026 we stopped using the "Carbon-Neutral" label on new EverTrail production while we review our claims.

### Divestiture

In August 2023 we sold our Quillmark Kids business to Bramblecrest Kids LLC for $18.0 million and recognized a loss on sale of $11.8 million, recorded in other income (expense). Under the sale agreement, the purchaser assumed all liabilities of the Quillmark Kids business, including litigation relating to periods before the sale, and agreed to indemnify us for them. The indemnity is supported by a $15.0 million escrow that runs to August 2027 and by a guarantee from Bramblecrest Holdings, Inc.

## Item 1A. Risk factors (selected)

- **We depend on Borealis Market for a large share of our revenue.** Borealis accounted for 34.8% of FY2025 revenue. Our vendor agreement expires at the end of 2026. Borealis could change its terms, fees or purchasing levels, which could reduce our revenue and margins.
- **Our Alder & Pine licence may be terminated in certain circumstances.** The licence may be terminated by the licensor if we materially breach it, or if our founder ceases to serve in specified roles without the licensor's approval of a successor.
- **We depend on our founder.** Ms. Achterberg leads our product design and our relationships with key partners. We do not have key-person insurance.
- **Our environmental and sustainability claims are subject to scrutiny.** Regulators and consumer groups are increasingly challenging environmental marketing claims.
- **Restrictions on chemicals in our products could affect our ability to sell certain inventory.**
- **A disruption at our Cedar Hollow distribution center could harm our business.**

## Item 3. Legal proceedings

- **FTC inquiry.** In November 2025 we received a civil investigative demand from the Federal Trade Commission about environmental marketing claims for our EverTrail products. We are cooperating.
- **Dealer litigation.** In June 2025, Summit & Spoke Outfitters LLC and 37 other former specialty dealers sued us in the U.S. District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, alleging that our March 2025 terminations violated state dealer protection laws. We believe the claims are without merit.
- **Trademark.** On January 14, 2026 the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York dismissed with prejudice the trademark infringement claims brought against us by Quilmarque SAS, which sought $95.0 million. The time to appeal expired on February 13, 2026 and no appeal was filed.
- **Former subsidiary.** Sunvale Textiles Co. has sued Quillmark Kids LLC, our former subsidiary, for $38.0 million for cancelled supply orders. Bramblecrest Kids LLC has assumed the defense under the sale agreement indemnity.

## Item 7. Management's discussion and analysis (excerpts)

**Revenue** grew 8.3% to $678.0 million. Footwear grew 13.9%. Borealis revenue grew 20.4% to $236.0 million.

**Gross margin** was 50.0%, up from 49.0%, mainly due to channel mix.

**Adjusted EBITDA** (a non-GAAP measure) was $128.0 million, up 14.3%. Adjusted EBITDA adds back share-based compensation and items management considers non-recurring, which in FY2025 were $2.4 million of store closure costs for two stores.

**Inventories** increased 21.4% to $238.0 million to support growth in our marketplace channel and new product launches.

**Liquidity.** At December 31, 2025 we had cash of $64.0 million, a term loan of $250.0 million maturing in September 2030, and $30.0 million drawn on our $100.0 million revolving credit facility, which matures in September 2029. Our credit agreement requires a maximum net leverage ratio of 3.75x. We were in compliance at December 31, 2025, with net leverage of about 1.7x. A change of control is an event of default under the credit agreement.

## Item 8. Notes to consolidated financial statements (excerpts)

### Note 2. Revenue recognition

We recognize wholesale revenue, including sales to Borealis, when control passes to the customer on delivery. We estimate returns, including returns under the Borealis stock rotation right, as variable consideration using historical return rates. Historical Borealis returns have averaged 3.1% of purchases. The returns reserve for Borealis was $2.6 million at December 31, 2025 and is included in accrued liabilities.

**Gift cards.** Gift cards and merchandise credits are issued by Quillmark Gift Card LLC, a Delaware limited liability company. We recognize breakage in proportion to the pattern of redemption. Cumulative breakage recognized in revenue since 2011 is $34.8 million. We believe unredeemed balances of Quillmark Gift Card LLC are not subject to unclaimed property laws, and we have not remitted unredeemed balances to any state.

### Note 4. Inventories

Inventories are stated at the lower of cost and net realizable value. We maintain an excess and obsolete reserve based on the age of inventory: no reserve for items up to 12 months old, 25% for items 12 to 24 months old, and 60% for items over 24 months old, adjusted for expected clearance recovery.

Apart from the two stores closed in FY2025, we recorded no impairment.
