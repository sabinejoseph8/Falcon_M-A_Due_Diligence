### Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On July 22, 2026, Quillmark Trail Co. (the "Company") entered into an Amended and Restated Vendor Agreement (the "Amended Agreement") with Borealis Market Inc. ("Borealis"). The Amended Agreement replaces the existing vendor agreement, which was due to expire on December 31, 2026. It takes effect on January 1, 2027 and runs to December 31, 2029.

Under the Amended Agreement:

1. Borealis will designate the Company as a "Featured Brand" in the outdoor category, with priority placement in search results and seasonal campaigns.
2. The co-operative marketing fee the Company pays Borealis will increase from 6.0% to 10.5% of Borealis's gross purchases from the Company.
3. The Company's wholesale prices to Borealis will be reduced by 6.0% across all products.
4. Borealis's stock rotation right will increase from 20% to 30% of its purchases in the two most recent fiscal quarters.
5. Borealis's minimum annual purchase commitment of $150.0 million will be removed.

The foregoing description is qualified in its entirety by the Amended Agreement, which will be filed as an exhibit to the Company's next quarterly report.
