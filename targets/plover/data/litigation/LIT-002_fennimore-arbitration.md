## Claims (from the demand for arbitration)

1. **Underpaid royalties.** Fennimore alleges that from 2021 to 2025 Quillmark calculated royalties on Alder & Pine "Net Sales" after deducting (a) co-operative marketing fees paid to Borealis Market, (b) retailer chargebacks and (c) markdown allowances. Fennimore says Section 1.12 of the licence expressly forbids deductions for "co-operative marketing, promotional allowances, chargebacks." It claims $11.6 million of underpaid royalties, based on a royalty audit by Kessling Royalty Audit LLP.
2. **Material breach.** Fennimore seeks a declaration that the underpayment is a material breach under Section 14.1 of the licence. The demand states that if Quillmark does not pay the amount found due within 60 days of an award, Fennimore "will be entitled to terminate the License Agreement."
3. **Reservation of rights.** The demand states that Fennimore "reserves all rights under Section 14.3 (Key Person) and every other provision of the License Agreement in connection with any transaction involving the Licensee."

## Quillmark's answer (July 2026)

Quillmark denies the claims. It says the deductions reflect "industry practice" and were "known to Fennimore for years." It has not asserted that Fennimore agreed in writing to the deductions. Quillmark has not paid any amount under protest.
