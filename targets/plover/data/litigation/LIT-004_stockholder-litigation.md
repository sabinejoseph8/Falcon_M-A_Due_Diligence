## Allegations

1. **Unequal consideration.** Ms. Achterberg, the controlling stockholder with 71.8% of the voting power, will receive the same $24.00 per share as other holders plus $32.0 million under a four-year Consulting and Restrictive Covenant Agreement with Ridgeline. The fee is payable whether or not services are requested. The plaintiffs say this is a disguised control premium of about $3.08 per Class B share ($32.0 million divided by 10.4 million shares).
2. **Process.** The plaintiffs allege the special committee did not negotiate the consulting agreement and did not obtain a fairness analysis of it, and that the merger was not conditioned on approval by a majority of the unaffiliated Class A holders.
3. **Disclosure.** The plaintiffs allege the tender offer documents do not disclose how the $8.0 million annual fee was set or what services are expected.

## Relief sought

- A preliminary injunction against completion of the tender offer until (a) the consulting fee is shared with all stockholders on a per-share basis, or (b) corrective disclosure is made.
- In the alternative, damages for the class equal to the value of the alleged control premium.
- Attorneys' fees.
