# LIT-003: Summit & Spoke Outfitters LLC et al. v. Quillmark Trail Co.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Court | U.S. District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin |
| Case number | 3:25-cv-00611 |
| Filed | June 23, 2025 |
| Plaintiffs | Summit & Spoke Outfitters LLC and 37 other former Quillmark specialty dealers in five states |
| Defendant | Quillmark Trail Co. |
| Status | Motion to dismiss denied April 2, 2026. Fact discovery closes January 29, 2027. Trial set for September 13, 2027. |
| Amount claimed | $33.5 million, plus reinstatement and attorneys' fees |

## Allegations

In March 2025 Quillmark ended the Authorized Specialty Dealer agreements of 141 dealers on 30 days' notice. The plaintiffs allege that the dealer agreements created "dealerships" under the fair dealership laws of their states, which require good cause and at least 90 days' notice with a chance to cure before termination. They allege Quillmark ended the agreements to move sales to Borealis Market and its own website, which is not good cause under those laws.

## Damages claimed

The plaintiffs' combined purchases from Quillmark were $24.8 million in FY2024. They claim three years of lost gross profit at an average 45% dealer margin: $24.8 million x 45% x 3 = $33.5 million. Several of the state statutes also allow attorneys' fees.

## Rulings

On April 2, 2026 the court denied Quillmark's motion to dismiss. It held that the plaintiffs had plausibly alleged a "community of interest" with Quillmark because they were required to carry minimum Quillmark inventory, use Quillmark fixtures and attend Quillmark training. The court did not decide whether good cause existed.

## Notes

Summit & Spoke Outfitters was one of Quillmark's larger specialty dealers. The other 103 terminated dealers are not parties. The limitations period for some of them runs to March 2027.
