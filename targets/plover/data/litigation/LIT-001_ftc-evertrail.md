## Allegations (from the complaint)

1. **"Carbon-Neutral" claim.** From 2022 to January 2026, Quillmark labeled and advertised EverTrail jackets, packs and accessories as "Carbon-Neutral." The complaint alleges that Quillmark relied entirely on credits from the Rio Verde Forest Carbon Project, and that a 2023 third-party report sent to Quillmark warned that the project's baseline was "materially overstated." The Global Offset Registry invalidated the project's credits in February 2026.
2. **"Made with 100% Recycled Nylon" claim.** The complaint cites FTC-commissioned laboratory tests of 14 EverTrail products, which found recycled nylon content of 29% to 46%. It alleges Quillmark relied on supplier certificates without testing.
3. The complaint alleges that both claims were deceptive under Section 5 of the FTC Act and that EverTrail products sold at an average price premium of about 22% over comparable Quillmark products.

## Relief sought (from the prayer for relief)

- A permanent injunction against unsubstantiated environmental claims, with a 20-year compliance order.
- **"Monetary relief, including refunds to consumers, in an amount equal to Defendant's net revenue from sales of EverTrail products from January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2025."**
- Civil penalties for any violations of the injunction after it is entered, up to the statutory maximum per violation.
- Costs.

The complaint does not state a dollar amount for monetary relief.
