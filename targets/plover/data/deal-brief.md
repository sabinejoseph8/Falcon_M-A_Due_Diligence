# Project Plover: deal brief

**Prepared for:** Investment Committee, Westbrook Lane Capital Partners IV, L.P.
**Prepared by:** Deal team (consumer and retail)
**Date:** October 16, 2026
**Status:** Confidential. Go-shop diligence in progress.

## 1. The situation

Quillmark Trail Co. ("Quillmark", Nasdaq: QTRL) is a US outdoor apparel and footwear brand based in Ashgrove, Oregon. On September 14, 2026 Quillmark signed a merger agreement with Ridgeline Brands Group, Inc., a strategic buyer, under which Ridgeline will make a cash tender offer of **US$24.00 per share** for all Class A and Class B shares.

The merger agreement includes a **60-day go-shop period that ends at 11:59 p.m. Eastern on November 13, 2026**. During the go-shop, Quillmark may solicit and negotiate a superior proposal. If Quillmark terminates the Ridgeline agreement to accept a superior proposal from a party that engaged during the go-shop, it pays Ridgeline a reduced break fee of US$18.7 million. After the go-shop the break fee rises to US$43.7 million.

Westbrook Lane was contacted by Quillmark's financial adviser on September 21, 2026 and has had data room access since September 28, 2026. This is a **topping bid**: we would need to submit a fully financed superior proposal before the go-shop ends.

## 3. Investment thesis (deal team view)

1. A founder-led brand with three growing segments: Apparel, Footwear and the licensed Alder & Pine heritage brand.
2. Strong online growth through the Borealis Market marketplace, which the deal team expects to keep growing at about 12% a year.
3. Margin upside: H1 2026 gross margin of 53.0% is the highest in the company's history.
4. A professionalized management team. Our plan is for founder and CEO **Marisol Achterberg** to become non-executive Chair at closing, with a new CEO from Westbrook Lane's operating partner bench. Ms. Achterberg has told the company she wants to step back from day-to-day management.
5. Value creation from closing underperforming stores and improving inventory turns.

## 4. Key facts about the target

- Founded in 2009 by Marisol Achterberg. Initial public offering in 2018.
- Dual-class shares: each Class B share has 10 votes. Ms. Achterberg holds all 10.4 million Class B shares.
- Reporting segments: Apparel, Footwear and Alder & Pine (a brand licensed from Fennimore Heritage LLC).
- Channels: direct-to-consumer (e-commerce and 46 own stores), the Borealis Market marketplace, and wholesale and specialty dealers.
- Main distribution center: Cedar Hollow, Linwood County, Oregon.
- Management's FY2026 outlook: revenue of about US$720 million and Adjusted EBITDA of about US$152 million.

## 5. Questions for the Investment Committee

1. **Earnings quality:** Is LTM Q2 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of US$141.0 million a reliable basis for valuing Quillmark?
2. **Durability of revenue:** How durable are Quillmark's sales channels and its Alder & Pine brand, especially after a change of management at closing?
3. **Legal and regulatory exposure:** What legal, regulatory and compliance exposures does Quillmark face, and how large could they be?
4. **Balance sheet:** Are inventory and the other key balance sheet items fairly stated, and what liabilities sit outside net debt?
5. **Deal execution:** What could prevent or delay a Westbrook Lane topping bid from closing, and how does our management plan interact with Quillmark's contracts?
6. **Price and protections:** Should we bid, and if so at what price and with what conditions, specific indemnities or other protections?
