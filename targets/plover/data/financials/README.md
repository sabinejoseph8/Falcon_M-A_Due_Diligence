# Quillmark Trail Co.: financial data pack

Prepared from the company's virtual data room, October 2026. All amounts are US$ millions unless a row says otherwise. The fiscal year is the calendar year. Half-year (H1) columns cover January to June. FY2022 to FY2025 are audited; H1 2025 and H1 2026 are unaudited.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `income_statement.csv` | Income statement, FY2022 to FY2025 and H1 2025, H1 2026. Revenue is shown by reporting segment. |
| `balance_sheet.csv` | Balance sheet at December 31, 2023, 2024 and 2025, and at June 30, 2026 (`Q2_2026`). |
| `cash_flow.csv` | Cash flow statement, FY2023 to FY2025 and H1 2025, H1 2026. |
| `adjusted_ebitda_reconciliation.csv` | Net income to EBITDA to Adjusted EBITDA as defined by the company, with the detail of "other non-recurring items". `LTM_Q2_2026` = FY2025 minus H1 2025 plus H1 2026. |
| `revenue_quarterly.csv` | Total revenue by quarter, Q1 2023 to Q2 2026. |
| `revenue_by_customer.csv` | Revenue by named customer for FY2024 and FY2025, ranked by FY2025 revenue. Shares are of total revenue. Direct-to-consumer sales are shown as one line. |
| `kpis.csv` | Operating metrics. The last column is H1 2026 for flow metrics and June 30, 2026 for point-in-time metrics. |

Notes from management:

- Revenue by channel in `kpis.csv` adds up to total revenue in each period.
- "Inventories, net" on the balance sheet is stated after the excess and obsolete inventory reserve shown in `kpis.csv`.
- Royalties paid to the Alder & Pine licensor are included in cost of revenue.
- Co-operative marketing fees paid to Borealis Market are included in selling, general and administrative expense.
- Special items in the income statement equal the operating items in the "Detail:" rows of the Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation. The FY2023 loss on sale of the Quillmark Kids business is in other income (expense).
