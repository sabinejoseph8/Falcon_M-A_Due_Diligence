# Down supplier regains certification after re-audit

**Outdoor Industry News** (trade publication) | May 29, 2026 | By Petra Lindgaard

Hengli Down Processing, a supplier of goose and duck down to several outdoor brands, has had its Responsible Down Standard certificate reinstated after a re-audit, the certification body said this week.

The certificate was suspended in March because transaction certificates for some batches were filed late. The re-audit found no animal welfare violations.

Brands that buy from Hengli include Quillmark Trail Co. and two other US outdoor labels. Quillmark said its products' down claims were not affected.
