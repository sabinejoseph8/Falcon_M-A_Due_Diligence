# Lab tests find EverTrail jackets fall short of "100% recycled" claim

**Gear Review Weekly** (consumer and trade magazine) | March 18, 2026 | By Jonah Pettibone

Six Quillmark EverTrail jackets bought at retail last autumn contained between 31% and 44% recycled nylon, with an average of 38%, according to laboratory tests commissioned by the Outdoor Integrity Project, a nonprofit that reviews environmental claims in the outdoor industry.

EverTrail products carry a "Made with 100% Recycled Nylon" label. The tests used a method that identifies chemical markers left by recycling. The Outdoor Integrity Project said it shared its results with the Federal Trade Commission, which it understands has been examining EverTrail marketing since late 2025.

Quillmark said its fabric supplier provides certificates showing 100% recycled content and that "testing methods for recycled content are not yet standardized."

The Outdoor Integrity Project receives some funding from member brands, which it does not name. Gear Review Weekly could not independently verify the test results.
