# Quillmark shareholders sue to block Ridgeline deal over founder's $32 million consulting fee

**Business Daily** (national business newspaper) | October 7, 2026 | By Simone Harcastle

Class A shareholders of Quillmark Trail Co. have sued in the Delaware Court of Chancery to block Ridgeline Brands Group's $24-a-share tender offer, arguing that founder Marisol Achterberg is getting more than other shareholders.

The complaint says Ms. Achterberg's $32.0 million, four-year consulting agreement with Ridgeline, which pays her whether or not she does any work, is "a disguised control premium" worth about $3.08 for each of her 10.4 million Class B shares. The plaintiffs ask the court to stop the tender offer until the payment is shared with all shareholders or disclosed more fully. A hearing on the request for a preliminary injunction is set for November 6, a week before the offer is due to expire.

"Courts often order more disclosure rather than stopping a deal outright," said Elena Castellanos-Reed, a corporate law professor. "But any bidder who offers the founder a side arrangement will face the same argument."

Quillmark and Ridgeline said the claims were without merit.
