# Borealis Market signals tougher terms for brands in 2027

**Retail Ledger** (trade publication) | November 12, 2025 | By Corinne Abelard

Borealis Market plans to "reset vendor economics" as its brand contracts come up for renewal over the next 18 months, chief financial officer Nadia Krol told analysts on the company's third-quarter earnings call.

"Brands that want featured placement will invest more with us," Ms. Krol said. She said Borealis wants vendors to fund a larger share of on-platform marketing and to "share the cost of price competitiveness" through lower wholesale prices. She also said Borealis will "reduce our exposure to inventory risk" and lean more on return-to-vendor rights.

Borealis did not name individual brands. Analysts at Pemberton Securities estimated that the changes could reduce gross margin on Borealis sales by four to eight percentage points for mid-sized brands.

Borealis is the largest online marketplace for outdoor goods in the US. Outdoor brands including Quillmark Trail Co., which makes about a third of its sales through Borealis, have vendor agreements expiring in 2026 or 2027.
