# Registry cancels 2.1 million carbon credits from Rio Verde forest project

**The Climate Desk** (online news) | February 9, 2026 | By Mateus Ferrante

The Global Offset Registry has invalidated 2.1 million carbon credits issued to the Rio Verde Forest Carbon Project after a review found that the project's baseline deforestation rate was overstated by about four times.

The registry said in a statement that the credits "do not represent real emission reductions" and that buyers who retired them "should not rely on them for neutrality claims." The review covered credits issued from 2021 to 2025.

Buyers listed in the registry's public retirement records include airlines, a coffee chain and outdoor apparel maker Quillmark Trail Co., which retired about 180,000 Rio Verde credits from 2022 to 2025. Quillmark uses the credits to support the "Carbon-Neutral" label on its EverTrail jackets and packs.

A Quillmark spokesperson said the company "bought credits in good faith from a project verified at the time" and was "reviewing its claims." Rio Verde's developer, Selvamar Carbon S.A., said it would appeal.
