# Borealis tells outdoor vendors to expect returns as it cuts stock

**Retail Ledger** (trade publication) | July 30, 2026 | By Corinne Abelard

Borealis Market has told outdoor vendors that it will cut the inventory it holds to no more than eight weeks of supply per brand by the end of 2026 and will "use contractual return-to-vendor rights where necessary," according to a memo seen by Retail Ledger.

A Borealis vendor scorecard dated June 30, also seen by Retail Ledger, shows several outdoor brands well above that level. Quillmark Trail Co. was among the highest, at more than 14 weeks of supply.

Borealis said it "works closely with brand partners to keep assortments fresh." Quillmark did not respond to a request for comment.
