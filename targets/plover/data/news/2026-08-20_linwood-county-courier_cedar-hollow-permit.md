# County staff back limits on Quillmark warehouse hours

**Linwood County Courier** (local newspaper) | August 20, 2026 | By Rosalind Treadway

Linwood County planning staff have recommended renewing the permit for Quillmark Trail Co.'s Cedar Hollow distribution center, but only with operations limited to 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. and no more than 180 truck trips a day.

The center now runs around the clock from October to December, with up to about 420 truck trips a day at the peak. The Cedar Hollow Neighbors Alliance, which has opposed the renewal since March, says night traffic on Hollow Creek Road is unsafe.

"Twenty-four-hour operation in peak season is essential," said Cedar Hollow general manager Arturo Vasquez-Lind. "If we lose it, we would have to look at a second facility." The county's economic development office estimated last year that a comparable second center would cost about $45 million to build and equip.

The county Land Use Hearings Board will hold a public hearing on November 18. The current permit expires on December 31.
