# Fountain pen maker Quillmark Pens enters administration

**London Business Post** | October 2, 2026 | By Harriet Ollerenshaw

Quillmark Pens Ltd, a family-owned fountain pen maker based in Birmingham, has entered administration after failing to refinance a bank loan of about £6 million. Administrators from Fairley Grant said they were seeking a buyer for the business and its 140-year-old brand.

The company, which has no connection to any other business using the Quillmark name, recalled 12,000 pens in 2025 after customers reported leaking ink cartridges.
