# Port Selang customs broker director held in bribery probe

**Asia Trade Monitor** (regional business news) | May 6, 2026 | By Wen-Li Corsaro

Anti-corruption investigators at Port Selang have detained Lionel Sabarre, a director of customs broker Meridian Trade Facilitation Co., on suspicion of paying customs officers to speed up clearance and to reclassify footwear shipments into lower-inspection categories.

Investigators seized ledgers showing "facilitation payments" over several years, according to a person familiar with the case. Two customs officers were also detained.

Meridian clears shipments for several Western footwear and apparel brands. The investigators did not name any of Meridian's clients, and there is no suggestion that any brand has been charged. Meridian did not respond to requests for comment.
