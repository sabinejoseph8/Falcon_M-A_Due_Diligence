# Ridgeline to buy Quillmark for $24 a share; founder ready to "hand over the keys"

**Business Daily** (national business newspaper) | September 15, 2026 | By Simone Harcastle

Ridgeline Brands Group agreed to buy outdoor brand Quillmark Trail Co. for $24.00 a share in cash, a 23.7% premium to Quillmark's closing price of $19.40 on September 11.

In an interview, founder and chief executive Marisol Achterberg said she was ready to give up running the company. "I've been CEO for seventeen years. I'm ready to hand over the keys," she said. "I want to spend my time on the Achterberg Foundation and our river restoration work. I'll be available as an adviser, but I won't be designing collections."

Ridgeline chief executive Paul Mendrano said Ridgeline would keep the Alder & Pine brand, which Quillmark licenses from Fennimore Heritage LLC. Augustus Fennimore, managing member of Fennimore Heritage, declined to say whether the licensor had been consulted about the deal. "We have rights under our agreement and we will look after the brand," he said.

The deal includes a 60-day go-shop period. Analysts at Pemberton Securities said a private equity bid "cannot be ruled out."
