# Bargain Ridge snaps up Quillmark surplus at deep discount

**Off-Price Insider** (trade newsletter) | June 22, 2026 | By Desmond Achebe-Ward

Off-price chain Bargain Ridge Outlet Group bought about 410,000 units of Quillmark Trail Co. jackets, fleece and pants from the 2024 and 2025 seasons this spring, paying roughly 28 cents for every dollar the goods cost Quillmark to make, according to two people familiar with the deal.

The purchase is one of the largest Bargain Ridge has made from a single outdoor brand. "Quillmark has a lot of older product sitting in the warehouse," one of the people said. "Prices for outdoor closeouts are the weakest I've seen in ten years."

Quillmark declined to comment on the deal. A Bargain Ridge spokesperson confirmed it "regularly buys from leading outdoor brands" but did not discuss terms.
