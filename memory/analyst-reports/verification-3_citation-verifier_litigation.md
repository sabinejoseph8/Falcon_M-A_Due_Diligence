### Most important corrections

1. **LIT-F03:** change "control weaknesses hidden" to "failed to disclose the full extent of control weaknesses". The complaint does not allege total concealment, and the 10-K disclosed a material weakness.
2. **LIT-F09:** "'094 never challenged" is wrong as written. It was not challenged at the PTAB, but Talonfield's counterclaims assert invalidity in court.
3. **LIT-F12:** the public injunction request names only Gen-3, not Gen-3 Plus. State post-trial exposure as conditional on Vantor prevailing.
4. **LIT-F13 and LIT-F03:** the class period is "proposed". Neither should be written as a settled fact.
5. **LIT-F02:** the PTAB '812 decision is "expected" in November 2026, not court-scheduled. Mediation has no set day.
6. **LIT-F08:** confidence can be raised to High, because I confirmed the 10-Q text from the primary source. In the report, describe the 10-Q as omitting the Markman order, not as misstating the case's stage.
7. **Severity and duplication:** LIT-F02 overlaps LIT-F10, LIT-F13 overlaps LIT-F03, and LIT-F11 and LIT-F12 are parts of LIT-F01. Consolidate them so the Vantor exposure and the securities allegations are not each counted several times among the High red flags.
8. **LIT-F01 severity basis:** the over-10% test relies on the complaint's $140.0m pleaded floor (12.1%). The expert figure is 8.3%. Critical is still justified by the injunction risk to Gen-3 (about 70% of 2025 hardware revenue, per the FY2025 10-K), so cite the 10-K for that.
