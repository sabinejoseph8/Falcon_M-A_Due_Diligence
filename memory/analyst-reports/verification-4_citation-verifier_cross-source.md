### Most important corrections

1. **X-04:** Vantor is not "the #1 market player". The Calder report only says Talonfield ranks third, behind two larger rivals, one of which is Vantor. Fix this in both the register row and the card.
2. **X-07:** All the numbers check out, but "a default before closing is likely" goes beyond the evidence. It depends on a legal reading of a definition the 10-K only summarizes, on projections, and on when closing happens. On the company's own method the Dec 31 ratio at the guidance midpoint would pass at about 2.70x. Present it as a material, conditional risk. The Sep 30 figures cannot be recomputed from the data.
3. **X-06:** "Deteriorating across every indicator" is false. H1 2026 software revenue grew 14.6% and services 14.4%. The ARR comparison sets a six-month change against a full-year change. Book-to-bill is 0.89, not 0.88.
4. **X-10:** "Interim CFO for 7+ months" and "unremediated for 3 quarter-ends" are not supported. The sources show an interim CFO through Aug 6, 2026 (about 6 months), and the weakness documented at Dec 31, 2025 and Jun 30, 2026.
5. **X-05 and X-18:** The 10-Q MD&A does disclose the cyber incident, and the 10-K's Note 2 discloses the goods held in the warehouse. These are framing and materiality problems, not omissions. X-18 covers two filings, not three. The no-8-K point is confirmed only as of Apr 14, 2026, and the proxy excerpts are weak evidence of a gap.
6. **X-08:** The $28.6m is "up to", estimated as of Dec 31, 2025. The $9.6m cash severance is paid only if an executive is terminated without cause or resigns for good reason.
7. **X-02:** Soften "bonuses depended on it" to "the bonus payout was materially helped by these sales". Revenue is only half the bonus formula.
