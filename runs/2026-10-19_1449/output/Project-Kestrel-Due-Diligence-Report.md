## 2. Answers to the six IC questions

### Q1. Quality of earnings

No. Reported earnings are not large or recurring enough for the price or a 6.0x term loan. FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA of 125.3 includes a 14.2 favorable change in estimate, all in Q4 2025 and 11.8% of that quarter's revenue (SEC-01, FIN-20). Without it, FY2025 growth is 8.0%, not 11.7%, and part of that includes the Pell Ridge acquisition, whose revenue is not disclosed (FIN-03, SEC-18). The estimate sits under a material weakness and an adverse ICFR opinion (SEC-06), and the collection assumption behind it is in doubt as denials rose from 10.7% to 16.2% and DSO from 62.7 to 79.0 days (FIN-09, SEC-19; inference). Four add-backs (payer denial write-offs, billing transition, lab start-up and restructuring) appear in each period from FY2022 to H1 2026 (FIN-01). H1 2026 shows gross margin down from 43.0% to 38.0% and reported EBITDA down from 39.0 to 19.6 (FIN-04). Combined view: LTM 109.2 without the one-off and 79.3 without both (X-01). The 750.0 loan is 6.87x the first and 9.5x the second (X-12).

### Q2. Reimbursement and compliance

Exposure is high. Medicare is the #1 payer at 104.3 (24.2% of revenue) and Medicare-paid hereditary cancer panels are about 76.2 (FIN-12). CMS cut the 0419U rate from US$2,150 to US$1,505 (-30.0%) from January 1, 2027, and about 40% of non-Medicare OncoTrace revenue is priced off that rate (NEWS-03, SEC-24). The effect is about 29.0 to 30.3 a year, 23.2% to 24.3% of EV (projection, X-04). On fraud and abuse, the US intervened in the Whitcomb qui tam on medical-necessity and kickback counts for Medicare OncoTrace claims; the relator alleges 96.4 of single damages and 3.2 of speaker fees to 61 physicians (allegations, X-05). Two commercial payers added prior authorization, which raised denials (SEC-19, SEC-28).

### Q3. Legal and regulatory

- **FCA qui tam (X-05, Critical):** complaint-in-intervention due October 30, 2026. Trebled relator claim 289.2 (allegation) before penalties; exclusion risk. Needs a specific indemnity or escrow and a closing mechanism.
- **Genaro patent case (X-07, High):** IPR institution denied and claim construction lost; Genaro's expert claims 68.0 plus a 9% royalty or a permanent injunction against OncoTrace (32.1% of revenue). Trial February 8, 2027, before closing; redesign expected mid-2027 subject to New York State approval. Needs an IP escrow and an MAE and closing treatment of the verdict.
- **Data breach (X-11, High):** seven consolidated class actions and an open HHS OCR review (response due October 30, 2026).
- **Orvia (X-10, High):** the #3 payer can terminate on 60 days' notice on change of control; needs consent.
- No securities or derivative suit appears in the litigation records (LIT-15, not verified, Low).

### Q4. Capital structure

The balance sheet is not stable to closing. Covenant Consolidated EBITDA at June 30, 2026 is 90.2, giving 4.39x against a 4.50x limit with a cushion of 2.2; without the 14.2 one-off it is 5.36x (FIN-16). From September 30, 2026 the limit is 4.00x, which needs 99.0; projected leverage is 4.48x to 5.01x (projection, FIN-17, X-03). A breach is an event of default, and lenders may stop revolver loans and accelerate (SEC-26). Cash was 28.8, LTM free cash flow -8.2, and the revolver has gone from 0.0 to 35.0 (FIN-10). Change of control is itself an event of default, cured by repayment at closing (SEC-20). Net debt is 395.9 at June 30, 4.9 above the brief (X-15).

### Q5. Disclosure, controls and governance

Reliability is weak (X-06). The 10-K, filed March 5, 2026, said Corvane was "not aware of any pending government investigation that we expect to be material", 22 days after receiving a DOJ civil investigative demand; in the filings provided, the CID first appears in the 10-Q of August 5 (SEC-04, SEC-22). The 10-K omitted the December 15, 2025 IPR denial and described a compliance program overseen by a CCO who had left in January (SEC-21, SEC-23). The April 27 8-K said there was "no evidence at this time" of data theft, six days after an attacker publicly claimed 1.1 TB (NEWS-08, allegation) and three days after plaintiffs allege a forensic finding (LIT-14, allegation). The material weakness is not remediated (SEC-06). Incentives: the FY2025 bonus paid 150% on EBITDA including the one-off; excluding it, the payout would be about 56.9% (inference). The CEO is Chair and co-owns the headquarters landlord, at 1.2 a year above appraised rent to 2040 (X-13).

### Q6. Price and structure

US$24.00 does not hold. On LTM excluding the one-off, 13.2x implies US$19.95, before the 2027 Medicare cut (about US$7.31 to 7.64 a share), the FCA case, the Genaro case and the Veltrane wind-down. The financing condition is projected to fail (X-02), and the existing covenant is projected to be breached before signing (X-03). Required protections are in section 7.

## 5. Cross-source insights

What combining sources showed that no single source did:

1. **The financing and covenant tests were built on the one-off.** The 10-K discloses the 14.2; the financial data shows covenant leverage of 4.39x passes 4.50x only because of it (5.36x without); the term loan is sized at 6.0x a base that includes it (85.2 of debt rests on it) (X-01, X-03, SEC-30).
2. **The August 5 guidance is already too high.** It excludes the 2027 Medicare rate (8-K) and predates the August 27 Veltrane halt (news). Adding both gives projected LTM of about 83.0 at a Q2 2027 closing (X-02).
3. **The Medicare cut reaches commercial revenue.** The news gives the rate; the 10-K gives the 40% commercial linkage; the KPIs give the Medicare share. Together: about 30.3 a year (X-04).
4. **The DOJ investigation was known before the 10-K.** Court records show the CID served February 11 and the government calling the investigation "active" on March 2; the 10-K of March 5 denied any material investigation (X-06).
5. **The 8-K was contradicted within days.** An attacker claim (news, April 21) and an alleged forensic finding (court complaint, April 24) came before the April 27 "no evidence" statement (X-06).
6. **The rumors were right.** The February trade-press report of a DOJ demand was confirmed by the 10-Q and the court record; the Bearhaven speaker-fee claim matches the intervened Count II in substance, though its US$40,000 a year figure is higher than about US$7,500 a year derived from the complaint (inference) (X-05).
7. **Dated judgments no longer hold.** The October 1, 2025 goodwill test assumed stable Medicare rates and 9% growth at US$27.80; since then the rate fell 30.0%, H1 clinical revenue fell 1.9% and the price fell 52.9% (X-09).
8. **The trial lands between signing and closing.** The litigation docket sets the Genaro trial for February 8, 2027 and the redesign for mid-2027; the financial data puts OncoTrace at 32.1% of revenue (X-07).
9. **The backlog thesis rests on one trial.** The 10-K says Veltrane's work is mostly ATLAS-3 and terminable on 90 days' notice; news reports the halt; the filings predate it (X-08).
10. **Incentives line up with the one-off.** The bonus threshold of 110.0 equals the financing condition, and the bonus paid 150% on a figure that includes the 14.2 (X-13).

## 8. Appendix

### 8.1 Methodology

**Architecture.** A lead agent ran a Plan, Act, Observe loop with four read-only specialist subagents (SEC filings, news and media, litigation, financial analysis) and a citation verifier.
