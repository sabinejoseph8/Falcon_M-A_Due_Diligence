## 1. Executive summary

### Bottom line

**Proposed recommendation: Do not proceed at the current price.** The Investment Committee makes the decision.

US$24.00 per share is not supported by Corvane's current earnings. The committed financing would not fund on the company's own guidance, and a covenant default is projected before signing. Re-engagement could make sense only at a re-based price, with re-cut financing and specific protections for the government case and the patent case.

### Top five red flags

1. **The price rests on a base that no longer exists (X-019, X-012).** FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA of US$125.3m includes a one-off US$14.2m. Corvane's Aug 5, 2026 guidance midpoint is US$103.0m. On that guidance alone, 13.2x supports US$18.39 per share, 23.4% below the offer. Adding the 2027 Medicare cut gives US$11.09 (projection). The offer is an 83.2% premium to the undisturbed price of US$13.10 (Oct 2, 2026), not the 32.6% in the brief.
2. **The financing condition and the covenant are both at risk (X-003, X-017).** The term loan needs LTM Adjusted EBITDA of at least US$110.0m at closing. Even the top of guidance (US$106m) is below it. A Q2 2027 closing LTM projects to US$72.9m to 106.0m (projection). The existing covenant steps down to 4.00x at Sep 30, 2026. Estimated net leverage is about 4.83x (projection, on an excerpted definition). The compliance certificate is due about Nov 14, 2026, before signing.
3. **Reported earnings overstate recurring earnings (X-001, X-015, X-014).** The US$14.2m Q4 2025 estimate change made up 11.3% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA. Add-backs that recur every year (denial write-offs, billing transition, lab start-up) add another US$18.9m. The estimate sits under an unremediated material weakness, while denials rise and collections fall.
4. **The flagship product's Medicare price is cut 30% from Jan 1, 2027 (X-004, X-010).** CMS cut the OncoTrace rate from US$2,150 to US$1,505. That is about US$29.0m a year of revenue and EBITDA (projection), 28.2% of the FY2026 guidance midpoint. Guidance excludes it, and the model assumes flat rates.
5. **The government has intervened in a False Claims Act case on the same product (X-005, X-006).** DOJ intervened on Aug 18, 2026 on medical-necessity and kickback counts for Medicare OncoTrace claims. The relator alleges US$96.4m of single damages, US$289.2m trebled (allegation). The FY2025 10-K did not mention the DOJ demand served 22 days before it was filed.

Also Critical: the loss of the Veltrane ATLAS-3 work (X-011) and the earnings trend (X-002). Also High: the Genaro patent trial before closing (X-009), cyber disclosure and exposure (X-007, X-008), and incentives and the related-party lease (X-016).

### Positive and mitigating points

- **Debt maturities:** none before closing. The revolver matures October 2028 and the term loan October 2029 (SEC-010). Net debt of US$391.0m ties to the brief (SEC-011, FIN-020).
- **Customer retention:** lab informatics gross revenue retention is steady at 95% to 96% (FIN-040).

## 2. Answers to the Investment Committee's questions

### Q1. Quality of earnings

**No. Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA are partly non-recurring, and current earnings are well below the base used for the price and the 6.0x loan.**

- The Q4 2025 change in the implicit price concession estimate added US$14.2m to revenue and EBITDA (X-001, SEC-001). Without it, FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA is US$111.1m and revenue growth is 8.0%, not 11.7% (FIN-049, FIN-050). The 8.0% still includes the Pell Ridge acquisition, whose revenue is not disclosed (SEC-021, FIN-045).
- Add-backs reached 30.2% of FY2025 and 44.8% of LTM Adjusted EBITDA. Denial write-offs (US$10.6m), billing transition (US$4.4m) and lab start-up costs (US$3.9m) recur every period (X-015, FIN-006 to FIN-009, NEWS-022).
- Cash conversion is weak. Operating cash flow was 22.7% of Adjusted EBITDA in FY2025 and 14.4% in H1 2026, and free cash flow is negative (FIN-023). DSO rose from 62.7 to 79.0 days (FIN-024).
- The trend has turned. Guidance was cut to US$100m to 106m, H1 2026 revenue fell 0.9% and gross margin fell to 38.0% (X-002).
- On guidance, the US$750.0m term loan is 7.3x Adjusted EBITDA, not 6.0x (X-003, FIN-066).

### Q2. Reimbursement and compliance

**Exposure is high and concentrated on one product.**

- Medicare fee-for-service is the largest payer: rank 1, US$104.3m, 24.2% of FY2025 revenue (SEC-018, FIN-030).
- The 0419U OncoTrace rate falls 30% on Jan 1, 2027. About 40% of non-Medicare OncoTrace revenue is priced off that rate (10-K). The effect is about US$29.0m a year, or 23.2% of EV at 13.2x (projection) (X-004, FIN-058). Management did not quantify it (NEWS-005).
- Payer behavior is worsening. The initial denial rate rose from 8.9% (FY2023) to 16.2% (H1 2026), and two commercial payers added prior authorization (SEC-025, FIN-037).
- Fraud and abuse: DOJ has intervened in the Whitcomb qui tam on Medicare OncoTrace billing and speaker fees (X-005). Potential outcomes include treble damages, per-claim penalties, Medicare exclusion and a Corporate Integrity Agreement (LIT-002). Legal and regulatory costs added back rose to US$4.8m in H1 2026 (FIN-010).
- OncoTrace (US$138.5m, 32.1% of revenue) carries the rate cut, the qui tam and the patent case at the same time (X-010).

### Q4. Capital structure

**Not stable. A covenant default is projected before signing.**

- The maximum net leverage ratio stepped down from 4.50x to 4.00x at Sep 30, 2026. Covenant EBITDA caps restructuring and non-recurring add-backs at 15% (SEC-007, SEC-008).
- On the financial analyst's estimate, net leverage was about 4.39x at Jun 30, 2026. That passes 4.50x, but only because of the US$14.2m (5.36x without it). The Sep 30 central estimate is about 4.83x against 4.00x (projection) (X-017, FIN-055 to FIN-057). The same method gives 4.89x at Dec 2023, which does not reconcile, so actual headroom may be larger. The CFO declined to give a leverage figure (NEWS-003).
- A breach is an event of default. Lenders could stop revolver draws (US$65.0m undrawn of US$100.0m) and accelerate the US$424.7m outstanding. Cash was US$28.8m at Jun 30, 2026 (SEC-011, FIN-022).
- A change of control is itself an event of default. The deal cures it by repaying US$424.7m at closing (SEC-009, FIN-020). No maturities fall before 2028 (SEC-010).
- Possible debt-like items sit outside net debt: the sale-leaseback lease liability, and an unexplained US$14.6m fall in other liabilities (FIN-021, FIN-047). Net working capital rose 80% in FY2025, so a normalized peg is needed (FIN-028).

### Q5. Disclosure, controls and governance

**Not reliable enough to rely on without specific representations.**

- The FY2025 10-K (Mar 5, 2026) said Corvane was "not aware of any pending government investigation that we expect to be material". That was 22 days after the DOJ demand (X-006, SEC-013). The 10-K also described the Chief Compliance Officer role in the present tense after the CCO had left (SEC-014, NEWS-008).
- The Apr 27, 2026 8-K described a "network disruption" with "no evidence at this time" of exfiltration. A ransomware group had publicly claimed theft six days earlier. Plaintiffs allege forensic findings three days earlier (allegation) (X-007, SEC-015, LIT-009).
- Controls: management concluded internal control over financial reporting was not effective, the auditor gave an adverse ICFR opinion, and the weakness was not remediated at Jun 30, 2026 (SEC-002, X-014).
- Incentives: bonuses are 100% Adjusted EBITDA. The Compensation Committee kept the US$14.2m in, and the bonus paid at 150%. Without it, the payout would have been about 57% (inference) (X-016, SEC-031).
- Related parties: the founder CEO is also Chair. His family entity leases the HQ to Corvane at US$4.8m a year against a US$3.6m appraised market rent, extended to 2040 (SEC-030, FIN-067).
- Change-in-control pay: CEO equity vests on a single trigger (US$31.4m), and the CEO has a US$3.5m 280G gross-up. Total change-in-control benefits are US$56.4m, including double-trigger items (SEC-032 to SEC-034).
- The Oct 1, 2025 goodwill test (14% headroom) is stale. The share price has fallen 52.9% since the test date (X-013).

### Q6. Price and structure

**US$24.00 does not make sense on current information.** Company guidance alone supports US$18.39 at 13.2x, and the projections go lower (X-019). The deal brief's assumptions are out of date (X-012). If the Committee wants to stay engaged, the minimum package is:

1. Re-base the price on post-guidance earnings, including the 2027 Medicare cut and the Veltrane outcome.
2. Re-cut the financing so the closing condition and sizing match current earnings. Define LTM Adjusted EBITDA and permitted add-backs in the agreement.
3. Make a waiver or amendment of the existing covenant, or evidence of Sep 30 compliance, a pre-signing requirement.
4. Put in place a special indemnity and escrow for Whitcomb and Genaro, and a specific indemnity for cyber.
5. Require Orvia's change-of-control consent as a closing condition.
6. Fix the related-party lease and redesign incentives.

## 5. Cross-source insights

These points appear only when sources are combined:

1. **The "record" year was built on a one-off that later analysis exposes (X-001, X-014).** The 10-K quantifies the US$14.2m. The newswire shows the CFO calling it a "refinement". 2. **The financing fails on the company's own numbers (X-003, X-017).** Combining the 8-K guidance, the deal brief's loan condition and the 10-K covenant definition shows that the closing condition fails and the covenant is projected to breach before signing.
3. **A trade newsletter sized the Medicare cut that the company left unquantified (X-004).** The 10-Q says only "evaluating the effect". The policy article gives the rate (US$2,150 to US$1,505), the 10-K gives the 40% linkage, and the KPI file gives the Medicare share. Together they size the cut at about US$29.0m a year.
4. **A customer's trial halt is a Corvane revenue event (X-011).** The Veltrane article does not name Corvane. The 10-K says most Veltrane revenue is from ATLAS-3, and the 10-Q puts US$61.0m of backlog on that program.

The docket now shows a CID served before the 10-K and government intervention. The 10-K said Corvane was not aware of a material investigation.

### 8.5 Limitations

- Litigation outcomes are uncertain. The damages figures are plaintiff and relator claims, not judgments.
- News items rated Low reliability are rumors or allegations unless a primary source confirms them.
- Q3 2026 actual results were not available.
