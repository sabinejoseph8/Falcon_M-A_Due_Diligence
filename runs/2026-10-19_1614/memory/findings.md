### X-001: The Q4 2025 change in estimate inflated FY2025
- **Finding:** In Q4 2025 Corvane changed its estimate of implicit price concessions. This added US$14.2m to FY2025 revenue and operating income, all in Q4 (10-K). The financial analyst's independent trend estimate of the Q4 excess (US$10.6m to 13.0m) is consistent with the disclosed amount. The CFO called it a "refinement of our collection estimates" on Mar 6, 2026 without an amount, and Bearhaven alleged it on May 19.
- **Numbers:** 14.2 / 125.3 = 11.3% of FY2025 Adj. EBITDA. At 13.2x: 14.2 x 13.2 = US$187.4m, which is 187.4 / 1,648.6 = 11.4% of EV.

- **Confidence:** High (10-K primary source; recomputed).
