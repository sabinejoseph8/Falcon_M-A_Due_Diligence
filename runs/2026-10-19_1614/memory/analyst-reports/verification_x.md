### Most important corrections

1. **X-009 severity:** recommend High. The card names "more than 10% of value" but no number it shows exceeds 10%. The permanent royalty is 9.5% of EV and past damages are 4.1%. The injunction is a possible outcome, not an event. Keep Critical only if it is restated as a block-closing test with the EBITDA loss shown against the US$110.0m condition.
2. **X-012 date error:** the CMS rate was published Jul 29, 2026, before the Jul 31 reference price. The Jul 31 price predates every listed event except the CMS cut.
3. **X-006 count error:** Feb 20 to Mar 5, 2026 is 13 days, not 14. Also, "first disclosed in the 10-Q" must say "in the available filings", because the Q1 2026 10-Q is not in the data room.
4. **X-003 and X-017 wording:** both state projections as facts ("fails", "compliance depended"). Label them as projections or estimates. X-017 must carry the Dec 2023 back-test caveat (4.89x does not reconcile).
5. **X-002 absolute word:** "across every measure" is contradicted by biopharma revenue growth of +2.7% in H1 2026. The volume and price-per-accession comparisons are against a FY2025 half-year average, not H1 2025.
6. **X-011 inference:** Veltrane did not name its vendors, so the effect on Corvane's contracts must be labeled an inference. Critical rests on a low end of 10.75% under no-mitigation assumptions.
7. **X-015 stale text:** remove "to be confirmed for overlap in iteration 2". FIN-050 has confirmed there is no overlap.
8. **X-008 and X-018:** label "larger than the insurance" as an inference. Add Tidewater Lab Properties Trust and Ellery Property Holdings LLC to the named-party check.
9. **Avoid double counting in value:** X-001 (11.4%), X-002 (17.5%) and X-019 (23.4%) partly measure the same thing. Use X-019 as the single value parent in the report.
