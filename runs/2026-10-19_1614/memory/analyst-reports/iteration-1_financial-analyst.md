#### FIN-001: Q4 2025 revenue spike; underlying FY2025 growth about 8.3% to 8.9%, not 11.7%
- **Finding:** Q4 2025 revenue of 120.5 is far above trend. Without an estimated 10.6 to 13.0 excess, FY2025 growth falls from 11.7% to roughly 8.3% to 8.9%.
- **Evidence:**
  - Reported growth = 431.0 / 386.0 - 1 = 11.7%.
  - Quarterly YoY growth in 2025: Q1 101.3/92.1 = +10.0%; Q2 103.9/95.4 = +8.9%; Q3 105.3/97.6 = +7.9% (decelerating); Q4 120.5/100.9 = +19.4%.
  - Q4 vs Q3 2025 = +15.2. Sequential steps from Q1 2024 to Q3 2025 averaged (3.3+2.2+3.3+0.4+2.6+1.4)/6 = 2.2.
  - Trend Q4 estimates: (a) Q4 2024 x 9M 2025 growth: 100.9 x (310.5/285.1 = 1.0891) = 109.9, excess 10.6. (b) At the Q3 rate: 100.9 x 1.0789 = 108.9, excess 11.6. (c) Q3 + 2.2 = 107.5, excess 13.0.
  - Underlying FY2025 growth: (431.0 - 10.6)/386.0 - 1 = 8.9%; (431.0 - 13.0)/386.0 - 1 = 8.3%.
  - Revenue then fell to 102.6 in Q1 2026, down 17.9 from Q4.
  - The H2 2025 over H1 2025 increase was 20.6 (225.8 - 205.2). Of that, clinical contributed 17.0 (165.9 - 148.9), biopharma 3.4 and informatics 0.2. The FY2024 H2 over H1 increase was 11.0 (198.5 - 187.5). This points to a clinical-segment item (inference).
- **Source:** `revenue_quarterly.csv` all rows; `income_statement.csv` rows 2 to 5 (FY2024, FY2025, H1 2025)
- **Severity:** High
- **Confidence:** Medium. The excess is my trend estimate. The actual one-off amount and its nature are not in the financials.
- **Deal impact:** Valuation. Thesis point 1 is overstated. The LTM Q2 2026 figure includes Q4 2025, but the LTM at closing (Q2 2027) will not (see FIN-016).
- **Cross-check:** The SEC 10-K or Q4 earnings release may name a one-time item, for example a payer settlement, a catch-up collection or a change in the implicit price concession estimate. News coverage of Q4 results is a second check. Lead: please send me any disclosed amount so I can replace the estimate.

- **Confidence:** Medium. The arithmetic is exact. Which items count as operating costs is analyst judgment. Scenario A alone is 10.6 x 13.2 = 139.9, or 8.5% of EV.
