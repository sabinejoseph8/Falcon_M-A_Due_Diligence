## 1. Executive summary

## 2. Answers to the six IC questions

### Q1. Is Talonfield's revenue growth real and sustainable?

Partly real, but lower than reported and slowing.

- Reported FY2025 growth of 19.1% includes $31.8m of Q4 bill-and-hold revenue. Without it, FY2025 grew 11.6% and Q4 2025 grew 5.1% instead of 32.4% (X-02, FIN-16).
- $9.4m of that revenue was returned and credited in Q2 2026 by the largest customer. The 10-Q says customers "pulled forward" orders into Q4 2025 (SEC-03, FIN-17).
- Since then: Q1 2026 +0.7%, Q2 2026 -4.0% (+6.0% before $11.7m of credits). Backlog is down 29.8% from Dec 31, 2024, NRR fell from 118% to 111%, and H1 gross margin fell to 38.0% (X-11).
- Guidance now implies FY2026 revenue of $470m to $490m, below FY2025's $502.3m (SEC-01).
- Allegations by a short seller and in a securities class action say the bill-and-hold sales were improper. These are allegations only (X-02, LIT-04).

### Q2. Are there legal exposures that could reduce value or block closing?

Yes, they could reduce value. None found that would block closing directly.

- **Vantor patent case (Critical):** the court adopted Vantor's construction of three of four disputed terms on Jul 22, 2026; the stay was denied; Vantor will seek a permanent injunction on Gen-3, about $211.0m (42.0%) of FY2025 revenue. Vantor claims "not less than $140 million" (12.1% of equity value); its expert puts royalty damages at $96.4m. Trial is Mar 8, 2027 and mediation is in December 2026. The company has no accrual and its Aug 6 10-Q did not mention the ruling (X-05).
- **Securities class action (High):** filed Jun 3, 2026 against the company, the CEO and the former CFO; amended complaint due Nov 9, 2026; no damages amount stated (SEC-12, LIT-04, LIT-05).
- **Pinecrest (Medium):** $12.0m claim over the April outage; company says its cap is about $1.4m; hearing Nov 5, 2026 (X-07, LIT-06).
- No matter seeks to enjoin the merger (LIT-08). The closing-related risks are contractual (Norvale, lenders), not litigation.

### Q3. How dependent is Talonfield on key customers, and are those relationships at risk?

Highly dependent, and the top relationship is at risk.

- Norvale Retail Group is rank 1 at $156.2m (31.1% of FY2025 revenue); top 10 customers are 61.5% (FIN-03).
- Norvale can terminate on 90 days' notice after a change of control, has no minimum volume, and its MSA ends Sep 30, 2027 (SEC-10).
- Norvale acquired robot startup Kinetic Aisle to "reduce our dependence on any single vendor" and plans a 2027 pilot (NEWS-07).
- $24.6m of Norvale's 2025 revenue was bill-and-hold, and the $9.4m return was with the largest customer (Norvale, inference). Norvale held 38% of receivables (about $46.2m) at Dec 31, 2025 (X-04, FIN-18).
- Named parties checked against the customer list: Pinecrest is rank 4 ($17.4m, 3.5%) and is suing; Harbor & Vine Grocers is rank 5 ($15.1m) and expanded in February 2026. Vantor, Kinetic Aisle, Delmarco and Greyfin are not among the top 10 customers (FIN-20, not verified).

### Q4. Are there balance sheet, debt or liquidity issues we would inherit at closing?

Yes. Manageable through refinancing, but tight before closing.

- A change of control is an event of default that lets lenders demand repayment of all $227.5m of debt, so it must be refinanced or waived at closing (SEC-07).
- The reported 2.26x leverage ratio appears to ignore the 15% cap on add-backs; with the cap it is 2.65x. The maximum fell to 3.00x from Sep 30, 2026. On guidance, the Dec 31, 2026 ratio projects to 3.06x to 3.56x (projection); the Sep 30 result is not in the data (X-03).
- Cash conversion has collapsed: OCF fell from $40.8m (FY2024) to $9.7m (FY2025) and -$4.2m (H1 2026); LTM free cash flow is -$26.4m (FIN-06). Liquidity was $59.7m at Jun 30, 2026 (FIN-19).
- Receivables rose 62.0% in FY2025 while the allowance was cut; the allowance was raised again by Jun 30, 2026 (X-08). Goodwill and intangibles ($132.5m) exceed book equity ($92.0m) (X-09).
- Transaction costs include $28.6m of change-in-control payouts, $17.6m of it single-trigger equity (SEC-16).

### Q5. Are there management, governance or internal control concerns?

Yes, several connected ones (X-06).

- The auditor was dismissed on Nov 10, 2025 after flagging a material weakness and saying Q4 non-standard revenue would need more audit work. The weakness (which covers bill-and-hold) was still not remediated at Jun 30, 2026 (SEC-04, SEC-05).
- The CFO resigned Jan 20, 2026 with a $900,000 payment; the 8-K says there was no disagreement, while anonymous sources report a dispute over Q4 revenue timing (rumor). The CFO role has been interim for about eight months (SEC-14, NEWS-05).
- 2025 bonuses paid at 142% of target; revenue without bill-and-hold ($470.5m) was just above the $470.0m threshold. No clawback policy appears in the proxy excerpts (SEC-15, SEC-27).
- Disclosure judgments look aggressive: the Vantor ruling was left out of the 10-Q (SEC-23) and the cyber incident was judged "not material" while a $12.0m customer suit was pending (X-07).
- An entity controlled by the CEO's family owns the HQ lease and can reset rent on a change of control (SEC-17).
- Adjusted EBITDA relies on add-backs (46.3% of LTM), with restructuring added back every year since FY2022 (X-10).

### Q6. What price adjustments or deal protections should we ask for?

See Section 6. In short: re-base the price on current earnings (11.5x on LTM or guidance points to $12.66 to $17.35 per share before synergies), make Norvale consent a closing condition, ring-fence Vantor with escrow or a specific indemnity (or wait for the PTAB decision and mediation), require Q3 2026 results and covenant certificates before signing, and obtain a quality of earnings report and forensic review of bill-and-hold contracts.

## 5. Cross-source insights

What combining sources revealed that no single source showed:

1. **The deal model was built after the guidance it ignores.** The brief dates model v3 to September 2026; the 10-Q replaced its basis on Aug 6, 2026 (X-01).
2. **The Q4 2025 "record" was bill-and-hold.** News reported a record quarter; the 10-K notes show $31.8m was bill-and-hold; the CSVs show growth falls to 5.1% without it; the 10-Q shows $9.4m came back (X-02).

4. **The 10-Q left out a court ruling made 15 days earlier.** Only the docket shows the Jul 22 claim construction loss (X-05).
5. **The covenant ratio does not match the credit agreement definition.** Applying the 10-K Note 9 cap to the CSV add-backs gives 2.65x, not the reported 2.26x (X-03).
6. **The largest customer has both a contractual exit and a strategic reason to use it.** The 10-K gives the change-of-control right; news shows Norvale buying a competitor; the CSV shows it is 31.1% of revenue (X-04).
7. **Dated accounting judgments did not survive later events.** The Dec 31, 2025 allowance cut was partly reversed by June 2026 (X-08); the Oct 1, 2025 goodwill test predates a 37.3% guidance cut and a 42.8% share price fall since March 2026 (X-09); the "not material" cyber conclusion came with a $12.0m suit already pending (X-07).
8. **Recurring "non-recurring" items.** News reports a fourth restructuring since 2022; the reconciliation shows add-backs in every period (X-10).

## 6. Recommended deal protections and price considerations

**Price**
- Re-base on current earnings. At the model's 11.5x: $17.35 per share on LTM Adjusted EBITDA, $12.66 to $14.56 on August guidance (X-01). Synergies ($35.0m by year 3) should be valued separately and not paid away in full.
- Value the premium against the pre-report price (about $15.36), not $17.85 (X-12).
- Price on normalized EBITDA after a quality of earnings review of add-backs (X-10).

**Conditions to signing or closing**
- Norvale consent to the change of control, or a waiver of its termination right (X-04).
- Q3 2026 financial statements and the Sep 30, 2026 covenant compliance certificate before signing; lender consent or refinancing commitment for $227.5m (X-03).
- Waiver of the HQ lease rent reset by the CEO-family landlord (SEC-17).
- Forensic report on the April 2026 cyber incident (X-07).

**Indemnities and escrow**
- Specific indemnity and escrow for the Vantor case, sized against the injunction risk and the $96.4m to $140.0m damages range (X-05).
- Specific indemnity for revenue recognition (bill-and-hold returns or restatement) and the securities class action (X-02, SEC-12).
- Cyber indemnity, including the Pinecrest claim (X-07).

**Structure**
- Earn-out or contingent value tied to Norvale revenue retention (X-04).
- Interim operating covenants: no buybacks, limits on revolver draws and new add-backs (FIN-12, X-03).
- Replace single-trigger equity vesting with retention awards (SEC-16); finance leadership plan (X-06).

**Timing**
- Consider extending exclusivity past Nov 20, 2026 to see the PTAB '812 decision, the Nov 5 Pinecrest ruling, the Nov 9 amended securities complaint and the December Vantor mediation (LIT-03).
