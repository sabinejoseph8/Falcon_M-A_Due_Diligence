### Most important corrections

1. **Severity downgrades to High (X-008, X-009):** Both name the "more than 10% of value" test but show only the value exposed. X-008 needs a loss of more than 49.8% of the US$310m Insights value; X-009 needs thermostat sales above US$325.8m a year. No source shows either. X-009's "could stop the deal" is asserted, not shown. Keep them Critical only if the lead can show that evidence.
2. **X-009 overstates the July 30 ruling.** The court denied a motion for judgment on the pleadings because limitations "turns on disputed facts". It did not reject the limitations defense.
3. **X-008 misstates the 2022 FTC Order.** Consent is required only for sharing that goes beyond the purposes disclosed at collection. The Ledger's 61% refers to records, not households.
4. **X-018 Nimbus inconsistency.** The simple case uses 82.0 against a net debt figure that excludes the 8.0. On a consistent basis it is US$320.5m and US$18.38 per share. The Calder royalty should be 20.1, not 20.2. The 30% WSEA margin is an unsourced assumption (also behind "10.4x" in X-017).
5. **Arithmetic fixes:** X-003 gives 183.7 (11.9% of EV), not 184.1. X-016's low-end gap is 0.9 points (US$7.4m), not 1.1 points (US$8.9m). X-020 should say all 8 assumptions are contradicted at least in part, not "7 of 8".
6. **Attribution and labels:**
   - "Lower warranty costs" is newsletter narrative, not a quote from the call. Cite the 10-K MD&A instead (X-004, X-014).
   - Gulf Horizon as the suspended distributor needs an "(inference)" label in X-015.
   - X-014's earn-out row should not say Meridale exclusivity existed at June 30.
   - "Uninsured" should be "appears excluded; insurer reserved rights" (X-011, X-020).
   - Severance is double-trigger, so it is a contingent payment, not one the deal itself triggers (X-017, X-018).
