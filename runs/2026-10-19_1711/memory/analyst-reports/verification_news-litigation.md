### Most important corrections

1. **LIT-002 should be High, not Critical, unless thermostat sales are shown.** The 10%-of-value test only works if Devices revenue is used as a proxy, and that includes sensors and hubs. Also fix "lost its limitations defense": the court only denied the motion on the pleadings because of disputed facts.
2. **NEWS-018 has primary support, so raise confidence to High.** 10-K Note 11 confirms the 120-day change-of-control funding duty. The buyout deficit is US$522.0m - 291.5m = US$230.5m (14.9% of EV, about US$4.40 a share). Use this figure instead of the retiree group's "well over $200 million".
3. **NEWS-023 has the wrong period.** 42.5% is the FY2025 gross margin, not Q4 (10-K MD&A, kpis.csv). The unhedged peso risk has already happened: H1 2026 margin was 39.4% (10-Q).
4. **NEWS-014 is confirmed by the 10-Q, so raise confidence to High.** From April 1, 2026, Delmora itself moved to 120-day terms with its main contract manufacturer. This added US$64.0m to accounts payable, 47.7% of H1 OCF of US$134.1m.
5. **LIT-008 and NEWS-001: the Aura claims look uninsured.** 10-Q Note 7 excludes battery thermal events in products made before 2024, which covers all Aura units. This supports keeping NEWS-001 Critical, but the US$192.8m must be called an upper bound.
6. **LIT-011 and NEWS-008: Reyes-Barrow is no longer CTO.** He left effective 2026-09-30 (8-K filed 2026-07-28). Update the wording and the key-person point.
7. **Smaller wording fixes:** NEWS-005 (61% of records, not households), NEWS-019 (91% of votes cast, not of 410 workers), NEWS-009 (the 60-day period ran to about April 5, after the article), NEWS-010 (the third-place ranking was not by those criteria; confidence Medium), NEWS-004 (remove "shows a pattern"), NEWS-021 ("challenged", not "contradicted"), LIT-003 (label the opt-in effect as a projection).
8. **For the SEC workstream (outside this batch):** the 10-Q filed 2026-08-06 says "We expect to be selected" by WSEA, 16 days after the 2026-07-21 award to Thermiq. It also says nothing about the Calder ruling of 2026-07-30.
