## Conflicts between sources

1. **Covenant ratio vs the 15% add-back cap.** The 10-Q reports Total Net Leverage of 2.26x at Jun 30, 2026. Back-solving (SEC-05) implies Consolidated EBITDA of about 89.6, almost equal to uncapped company Adj. EBITDA of 89.7 (FIN-01). But the 10-K Note 9 definition caps Permitted Add-backs at 15% of Consolidated EBITDA before those add-backs, and LTM add-backs are $41.5M (FIN-03). Resolved in iteration 2: 2.26x reproduces only with uncapped Adj. EBITDA; with the cap it is 2.67x (FIN-14), or 3.65x if SBC sits inside the cap. Legal reading of the cap needs counsel and the full credit agreement.
2. **Net debt.** SEC analyst assumed a $185.0M term loan (net debt about 196.1). The balance sheet shows term loan 182.5 and net debt 193.6 (FIN-01). Use the balance sheet.
3. **Bill-and-hold.** Company called the short report "misleading and inaccurate" (NEWS-02) but the 10-K had already disclosed $31.8M of bill-and-hold (SEC-02) and the 10-Q later recorded a $9.4M return. Company 10-K language "strong hardware deliveries" vs Note 2 "remained in our warehouses" (SEC-17).
4. **Auditor change.** 8-K says "no disagreements" (SEC-03) while the outgoing auditor had flagged a reportable event and an expanded Q4 scope. Company told press it was "a routine review" (NEWS-03).
5. **CFO departure.** 8-K says no disagreement (SEC-04); anonymous sources say he disputed Q4 revenue timing (NEWS-04, rumor).
6. **Cyber incident scale.** 10-Q: "some customer fleets"; news: 40+ sites (NEWS-05). Company "not material" vs $12.0M Pinecrest claim.
7. **Vantor status.** 10-Q (Aug 6) says "in discovery" (SEC-09); docket shows an adverse Markman ruling on Jul 22, 2026, before the 10-Q was filed (LIT-02). Resolved: the 10-Q does not mention it (SEC-20).

9. **FIN-20 arithmetic.** Verifier confirmed: guidance midpoints are 14.3% (revenue) and 37.3% (Adj. EBITDA) below the model. FIN-20 case (c) double-counted bill-and-hold; corrected range $13.61 to $17.35 per share.
