### Most important corrections

1. **SEC-09: covenant headroom may be negative, not $1.7M to $2.5M.**
   - When add-backs exceed the 10-K's 15% cap, covenant EBITDA = 1.15 x (Adjusted EBITDA minus restructuring and other non-recurring add-backs).
   - To pass 3.00x at Dec 31, 2026, with net debt held at $202.5M, covenant EBITDA must be at least $67.5M. Adjusted EBITDA minus those add-backs must then be at least $58.7M (67.5/1.15).
   - That allows FY2026 add-backs of only $11.3M, $15.3M or $19.3M at Adjusted EBITDA of $70M, $74M or $78M. H1 2026 add-backs were already $10.6M (4.4 + 6.2), and a 4% layoff was announced on Jul 30, 2026.
   - If stock-based compensation is also inside the cap, leverage at Jun 30, 2026 would be about 3.65x (202.5 / (48.2 x 1.15)). That is above the 3.50x limit and conflicts with the reported compliance.
   - Either way, the reported 2.26x does not follow from the definition as summarized. Get the credit agreement and the Q2 and Q3 compliance certificates before the IC, and have the financial analyst check this.
2. **SEC-22 and SEC-08: Vantor's demand was quantified from the start.** The March 2024 complaint seeks not less than $140.0M, plus enhanced damages for willful infringement and a permanent injunction (LIT-001). Neither the 10-K nor the 10-Q states the amount or the willfulness claim. Size the exposure on $140.0M (12.1% of equity value; $2.90 per share) as well as the $96.4M expert figure, and rate the combined Vantor risk Critical.
3. **SEC-07: what triggered the suit is in the file set.** The complaint cites the Nov 14, 2025 8-K and the May 12, 2026 Greyfin report (stock down 18%) as corrective disclosures. The proposed class period is Mar 10, 2025 to May 12, 2026.
4. **SEC-11: "likely" is too strong.** A missed EBITDA threshold depends on an undisclosed hardware margin above 34.0%. The revenue point stands: without bill-and-hold, revenue is just $0.5M above threshold.
