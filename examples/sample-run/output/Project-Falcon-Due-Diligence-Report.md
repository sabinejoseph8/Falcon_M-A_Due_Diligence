## 2. Answers to the Investment Committee's questions

### Q1. Is Talonfield's revenue growth real and sustainable?

Partly. Reported FY2025 growth of 19.1% included $31.8M of Q4 bill-and-hold revenue for robots still in Talonfield's warehouses at year-end. As a sensitivity excluding it, FY2025 growth was 11.6%, Q4 growth 5.1% and hardware growth 2.9% (X-01, FIN-19). $22.4M of those robots were delivered in H1 2026 and $9.4M were returned and credited (SEC-04). Underlying demand has slowed: hardware backlog fell 22% during FY2025 (FIN-08), H1 2026 revenue fell 1.7% as reported, and FY2026 guidance was cut to $470M to $490M (SEC-01). The software business is the durable part: FieldOS ARR was $146.8M at June 30, 2026, though retention slipped from 118% to 111% (FIN-08, X-15).

### Q2. Are there legal exposures that could reduce value or block closing?

Yes, one Critical and several High. The Vantor patent case could cost $96.4M to $140M or more (enhanced damages can reach three times actual damages, counsel to confirm) and Vantor will seek an injunction against Talon Gen-3 sales; the court adopted Vantor's construction of three of four disputed terms on July 22, 2026, and refused a stay (X-03, LIT-01 to LIT-03). The trial (March 8, 2027) could fall between signing and closing, depending on the timetable (LIT-05). A securities class action names the CEO and former CFO (LIT-06), and a top-5 customer is suing over the April 2026 outage (X-09). The Ruiz wage settlement ($3.2M) is accrued and low risk (LIT-11).

### Q3. How dependent is Talonfield on key customers, and are those relationships at risk?

Highly dependent, and the largest relationship is at risk. Norvale was 31.1% of FY2025 revenue and 38% of gross receivables (28.0% excluding its $24.6M of bill-and-hold, flat on FY2024). It can terminate on 90 days' notice after a change of control, has no minimum volume, returned $9.4M of product under a May 2026 amendment, and bought a robot startup in June 2026 "to reduce our dependence on any single vendor" (X-04, FIN-20, NEWS-07). Pinecrest, customer number four, is suing and "reviewing our options" (X-09). The top 10 customers were 61.5% of revenue (FIN-06).

### Q4. Are there balance sheet, debt or liquidity issues we would inherit at closing?

Yes. A change of control is an event of default, so $227.5M of debt plus $4.2M of letters of credit must be refinanced at closing (X-08). Before closing, the covenant steps down to 3.00x; on the credit agreement definition as summarized in the 10-K, leverage was 2.65x at June 30, 2026 (reported: 2.26x) and could reach 3.05x to 3.55x by December 31, 2026 on revised guidance (FIN-17, FIN-18). Free cash flow has been negative for the last 12 months (LTM -$26.4M), receivables and contract assets ballooned, and inventory rose 17.8% in H1 2026 (X-06, FIN-11). Liquidity was $59.7M at June 30, 2026.

### Q5. Are there management, governance or internal control concerns?

Yes, several, and they cluster around Q4 2025 (X-07). The auditor of nine years was dismissed in November 2025 after reporting a material weakness over hardware contracts with non-standard terms, including bill-and-hold, and saying Q4 arrangements with these terms would need extra procedures; the weakness was still unremediated at June 30, 2026 (SEC-05). Without the bill-and-hold revenue, revenue would have cleared the bonus threshold by only $0.5M (bonuses were paid at 142% of target) (FIN-21). The CFO resigned with a $900,000 payment; the 8-K says the resignation was "not the result of any disagreement with the Company", while two anonymous sources describe a dispute over Q4 bookings (a rumor) (NEWS-06). The CEO chairs the board with no lead independent director, holds 14.2%, and his family's company can reset headquarters rent after a change of control (X-11).

### Q6. What price adjustments or deal protections should we ask for?

See Section 6. In short: re-price on current earnings; address Vantor through price, a contingent value right or a closing condition; make a Norvale waiver and contract extension a condition; complete a forensic accounting review and get the credit agreement before signing; and extend exclusivity past the November court and PTAB dates.

## 5. Cross-source insights

These points only appeared when sources were combined, which is the job of the lead agent's Observe step.

2. **A court ruling the 10-Q leaves out.** The claim construction order went against Talonfield on July 22, 2026; the 10-Q filed 15 days later describes the case as "in discovery" and repeats "without merit" (X-02, X-03).

6. **A covenant ratio that does not follow its definition.** The 10-K's covenant definition, applied to the reconciliation data, gives 2.65x instead of the reported 2.26x (X-08).
7. **A small customer with a big lawsuit.** The $12.0M Pinecrest claim equals 69% of a year's revenue from that customer (X-09, FIN-22).

## 6. Recommended deal protections and price considerations

**Price**

- Re-base the price on current earnings. At the deal model's own multiple, current guidance supports $12.60 to $14.50 per share and LTM Adjusted EBITDA $17.27, against the $24.00 offer and the $17.85 undisturbed price (X-05).
- Treat the Vantor claims, restructuring costs and any covenant waiver fees as real costs or debt-like items, not add-backs (FIN-05, FIN-23).
- Even crediting all $35M of run-rate synergies today, the offer is 10.8x LTM Adjusted EBITDA (FIN-01).

**Structure and conditions**

- **Vantor:** a contingent value right or price adjustment tied to the outcome, a closing condition that no injunction against Gen-3 is in force, and Arbelion's consent before any settlement or license (counsel to advise; indemnities from public shareholders are rarely available).
- **Norvale:** a written waiver of the change-of-control termination right and an extension of the supply agreement beyond September 30, 2027, as a signing or closing condition.
- **Accounting:** a forensic review of Q4 2025 contracts and 2025 revenue cut-off before signing, with a no-restatement representation.
- **Debt:** the full credit agreement and the June and September 2026 compliance certificates before signing; a committed refinancing for closing; interim covenants limiting new debt, buybacks and restructuring.
- **Governance:** an independent special committee to run the process; a landlord waiver of the rent reset; a retention plan, given single-trigger vesting.
- **Cyber:** the forensic incident report, a specific cybersecurity representation and an insurance review.

**Timing**

- Extend exclusivity beyond November 20, 2026, to cover the Q3 2026 results, the PTAB decision on the '812 patent and the December Vantor mediation (X-10).
